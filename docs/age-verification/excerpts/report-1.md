## Misses, by cause

- **On documentation pages, the excerpt stops one sentence short of the fact (7, 19).** The extractor picks the sentence
  that matches the key's wording and stops there; the fact is in the sentence next to it. In 7 the year is in the
  preceding paragraph; in 19 it is the next step of the same instructions. Steps of one procedure should be kept together.
- **A table row loses its first cell (27).** In a two-column table (shortcut | action) only the action cell is kept.
  Item 17 shows the same pattern (level name dropped) without causing a miss.

## Flags, by cause

- **Spacing breaks where HTML pages have inline formatting (23, 49; cosmetic in 8, 9):** words run together
  («Win32_Processorclase»), a menu path reordered and joined, and stray spaces before punctuation.
- **BOE editorial notes inside the article text (16):** «Téngase en cuenta…» notes come from the consolidated PDF but are
  not part of the norm; they should be stripped.
- **Inconsistent handling of lettered and numbered sub-units (21, 26, 31):** 24 and 32 show the chapeau plus the cited
  letter, which is the right shape. 21 and 31 show the whole apartado, and 26 shows the bare list item with neither
  chapeau nor letter.
- **Documentation heading taken from the raw page title (7):** the site prefix «Portal Transparencia |» is kept.
