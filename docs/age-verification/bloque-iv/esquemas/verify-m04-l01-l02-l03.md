## Notes

- Choice (spec, «A list or table already in the prose is not repeated as an esquema»): in m04-l02, the arbol «Clases
  de órganos de selección» and the RD 364/1995 row of the exclusion table restate the bullet list at l. 77–84 (Tribunales,
  Comisiones Permanentes, art. 13.2). The rows themselves are correct (#3 aside), but this pair comes close to repeating
  a prose list. It is defensible: the arbol adds the art. 10 frame, and the table gathers the three norms' exclusions,
  two of which are prose sentences, not list items. It is not failed, but the ledger entry should say why the l. 77–84
  list was not left as the only form.
- m04-l02 #7: the TREBEP row leaves out «considerando como tales las definidas en el apartado 2 del artículo 4 del texto
  refundido […] RDL 1/2013». That is a definition by reference, not a narrowing threshold, and the prose gives it
  separately (l. 143–145), so the row does not read wider. This is different from #9–#10, where the 33% threshold is
  the norm's own narrowing condition. If the caller wants the three rows symmetrical, adding «para ser cubiertas entre
  personas con discapacidad» to rows #7–#8 too is optional.
- m04-l02 #9–#10 and m04-l03 #4–#5: each FAIL comes from a condition or exception that the prose also leaves out. The
  esquema copies the prose faithfully, so the fix is a prose decision first (add the clause with its citation), then
  the row. If the prose is not to change, the alternative is to remove the row rather than keep a row that reads wider.
- m04-l03 #3: art. 114.4 is cited in the prose (l. 150), but for the excepcional concurso. The prose does not repeat
  the 114.4 list «oposición, concurso-oposición y, excepcionalmente, el de concurso» as a sentence, though it describes
  both ordinary systems at 114.5 and 114.7. It is treated as already stated. A strict "sentence in prose" reading would
  want the 114.4 list in the l. 149–150 sentence.
- m04-l01 #4: the node follows the norm («modalidades de contratación de personal») where the prose leaves out «de
  personal». The node is more verbatim, not wider.
- Voice: labels and headers («Norma», «Se aplica a», «No pueden formar parte», «Reserva», «Sistemas selectivos»,
  «Regla») are plain Peninsular Spanish with no invented terms.
