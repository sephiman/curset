Input: `style-rewrites-i-v.json` (152 «rewrite», 1 «unsupported»). Rules: `style-fix-brief.md`. Each new explanation was read against its question in `<bloque>/mBB-lTT.draft.json`, and every claim beyond the key's quote was checked in the norm text (`pdftotext -layout` from the files in `sources/normativa.yaml`). This report rules on the `new` text. The drafts still hold the `old` text.

How the rules were applied:
- A distractor may be ruled out by the cited sentence's own figure, organ or rule when that sentence is quoted or paraphrased. Several distractors may share such a sentence when each is named and the quoted text contradicts each one directly.
- Absence from a list counts as a reason only when the explanation quotes or paraphrases the whole closed list, or the norm closes it itself («únicamente»). «No figura / no aparece / no está entre / tampoco» set against a list the explanation does not give, or against an open list, is dismissal by silence and fails.
- «Lo mismo vale para…», or one blanket sentence for several distractors with different contents and no sentence of the norm for each, fails as a reused sentence.
- A claim that does not hold on the norm fails.
