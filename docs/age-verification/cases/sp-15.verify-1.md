Mon 18, Fri 22, Thu 28, Fri 29 Jan; Mon 15 Feb; Thu 18 Mar 2027, and every stem date). Consistent: price 150.000

delivery precedes the registry entry; Hacienda is a department of the same AGE. Premises outside the lessons: the
Jefatura Central de Tráfico is an organismo autónomo adscrito al Ministerio del Interior (Ley 47/1959; BOE
resolutions of 2026 describe it so), with Jefaturas Provinciales as its peripheral services; art. 12.3 RD 1112/2018
lost only its basic character under STC 100/2019 («carecen de carácter de legislación básica»), so it still binds
the AGE and its organismos. Names (one company, nine persons) searched: no real match. Voice: no «never does» item.

Residual risk (no fault): if Toledo moves San Ildefonso off Saturday 23 Jan (to Fri 22 or Mon 25), the scenario's
festivos sentence and q02, q06, q14 would need a re-check when the city publishes its 2027 calendar.
