## Choices a verifier should look at

- **Month-based counts and intermediate festivos.** For plazos in months (q110, q111, q128, q129, q130, q131, q188) only
  the last day matters (art. 30.4–5). Some of those windows contain regional festivos (e.g. 24 June) or a national one
  that falls before the last day; none falls on the computed last day (in q131, 1 May follows the last day, 30 April,
  and only matters for the distractor that moves the plazo into May). Day-based windows hold no festivo from
  the spec's list (asserted in the generator), except the two that name it in the stem (q107: 19 March; q172: 6 January).
- **q103 (horas).** Counts all 24 hours of each día hábil (art. 30.1: «son hábiles todas las horas del día que formen
  parte de un día hábil»): Friday 12:00 + 24 h → Monday 12:00. Same reading as the official key of conv2024_X·S2·9.
- **q123 (urgencia).** The audiencia minimum of ten days halves to five under art. 33 (the only plazos excepted are
  solicitudes and recursos). Derived, not stated in so many words; drop if the verifier finds it arguable.
- **q144 (certificado del silencio)** counts the fifteen días hábiles from the day after expiry (art. 30.3, «desde el
  siguiente a aquel en que se produzca la estimación o la desestimación por silencio»).
- **q181 (alegaciones tras actuaciones complementarias)** counts from the notification of their finalización; art. 87
  says only «tras la finalización», so the stem states that the finalización is notified that day.
- **q188 (advertencia de caducidad)** counts the three months from the day after notification of the advertencia.
- Left out on purpose: a 30.6 computation (art. 31.3 excludes 30.6 for electronic registros, which the lesson does not
  say), computations of the suspension in art. 22 (dies a quo not fixed by the text) and of the informe in art. 80.2.
