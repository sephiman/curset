  average of 10 por 100 in the circunscripciones with representation, or 3 por 100 nationally. Both are tagged
  `cambio-pendiente`, and both stems say «en su redacción para la XVI legislatura». No distractor uses the earlier
  wording (15 / 5 por 100). The reason: someone who reaches the old 5 por 100 national threshold also reaches the

  Congreso used here is affected by the pending change.

  for the reform, so the transitional provision may not resolve as a citation on its own.
