## Choices and facts left out

- **Publication day in art. 114.12.** The lesson says the article does not say whether the publication day counts and
  applies art. 30.3–4 LPAC. Every date question starts the count the day after publication, and no distractor is the
  date you get by counting the publication day, because that reading is open.
- **Ending on an inhábil day.** No toma de posesión key falls on a weekend or festivo, because art. 30.5 LPAC is outside
  the lesson's citations. q016's «lunes 3 de mayo» distractor uses the move to the next working day only to explain how
  that wrong date is reached.
- **Jubilación dates.** Every birth date puts age 65 in 2027 or later, so the 2026 row of DT 7.ª LGSS never governs the
  key. 66 años y 10 meses appears only as a distractor (the 2026 row). The non-Régimen General questions (q058, q059)
  say «sin normas estatales específicas de jubilación» so that art. 67.3 applies in full.
- **TREBEP vs Ley 30/1984.** Prolongation questions on art. 33 Ley 30/1984 name the norm in the stem. q052 asks what
  art. 67.3 TREBEP adds (a motivated resolution). q053 asks about the BOE note. Its `source_quote` stops at «en tanto no
  se oponga a lo establecido en el mismo», because the note's remaining words run across a page break in the
  pdftotext output.
- **Left out because the lesson does not state them:** art. 64.3 TREBEP (renuncia does not bar re-entry), art. 68.2
  (exceptional rehabilitation after inhabilitación; q063 uses «con carácter excepcional» only as a distractor wrong
  under 68.1), the first paragraph of art. 114.12, the cotización rules of art. 205.1.a) beyond the ages, and the
  other DT 7.ª rows.
- **Official question not coverable in this range:** conv2020_X·S2R·1 (the organ competent to grant jubilación
  voluntaria). The lesson does not state it.
