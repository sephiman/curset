| caso:### Declaraciones censales#1 | PASS | Art. 11.4 RGAT gives the heirs six months from the death to file the baja and to report the change of ownership of rights and obligations still in force with third parties. Counting from Tuesday 3-11-2026 «de fecha a fecha» under art. 30.4 LPAC, the term ends on 3-5-2027, which is a Monday (checked with a calendar). The case leaves out the alta declarations («en su caso»), which is harmless. |
| caso:### Número de identificación fiscal#1 | PASS | Art. 19.2 RGAT requires its own NIF from a Spaniard resident abroad and from a Spaniard under 14 resident in Spain. When the Administración assigns it, the initial letter is L and K respectively, as the case says. |
| caso:### Libros registro#1 | PASS | A physiotherapist carries on a professional activity, so in estimación directa simplificada he keeps the four books of art. 68.5 RIRPF (ingresos, gastos, bienes de inversión, provisiones de fondos y suplidos). A business in modalidad normal whose activity is not mercantile is limited to the three books of art. 68.3. |
| caso:## Obligaciones y deberes de la Administración tributaria#1 | PASS | Under art. 32.2 LGT, interest is paid without a request and accrues from the date of the ingreso indebido (Tuesday 10-2-2026) to the date payment of the refund is ordered (Monday 20-7-2026). Both weekdays are correct, and the case states that there are no delays to exclude. |
| caso:### Obligados en las obligaciones entre particulares#1 | PASS | Art. 38.2 LGT: unless a law provides otherwise, the obligado a soportar la repercusión is the destinatario. That person does not pay the Administración but must pay the sujeto pasivo the cuota repercutida of 210 €. |
| caso:### Sucesores#1 | PASS | Art. 39.1 LGT: pending obligations pass to the heirs, subject to civil law on acquiring the inheritance, and sanctions never pass. The 600 € sanción therefore stays with the deceased, and the weekday of 3-11-2026 (Tuesday) is correct. |
| caso:### Responsables#1 | PASS | Art. 41.2 LGT makes responsibility subsidiaria unless an express legal provision says otherwise. Art. 41.5 requires an act declaring responsibility after audiencia and, for a subsidiario, the prior declaración de fallido of the principal debtor and of the solidarios. Minor point: the case puts «salvo norma con rango de ley» over both requirements, but the article states that proviso in the first paragraph, and this does not change the result. |
| caso:## La capacidad de obrar en el orden tributario#1 | PASS | The case follows art. 44 LGT: a minor has capacity in tax relations arising from activities the law allows without assistance. The case also states the exception for menores incapacitados correctly. |
| caso:### Representación legal#1 | PASS | Art. 45.2 LGT looks to who holds the representing body at the moment of the actuación. On Wednesday 10-3-2027 (weekday correct) that is Elena, who has held the post since 1 March. |
| caso:## Domicilio fiscal#1 | PASS | 30,000 of 50,000 is more than half of the base imponible general of the previous year (2026, judged in 2027), which meets art. 113 RGAT. «Podrá considerar» matches the discretionary power in art. 48.2.a) LGT to take the place where management is centralised instead of the residencia habitual. |
| caso:## Plazos de prescripción#1 | PASS | Art. 66 bis.2 LGT: the ten years run from the day after the end of the filing period, so from 1-7-2022 (30-6-2022 was a Thursday, correct). On 15-9-2026 the right has not prescribed. |
| caso:### Cómputo#1 | PASS | Art. 67.2 LGT: for a solidario the term runs from the day after the end of the voluntary period, which is Tuesday 21-4-2026, and the case states the condition that the facts are not later. For a subsidiario it runs «desde la notificación» of the last collection step, here Monday 4-5-2026 (weekdays correct). «Empieza ese día» follows the literal wording, which unlike the other rules does not say «día siguiente». |
| caso:### Interrupción#1 | PASS | Under art. 68.2.a) LGT, an action aimed at collection and known formally to the debtor interrupts the term. Art. 68.8 extends the interruption to all other obligados unless the debt is mancomunada, which the case excludes, and limits it to the debt the action refers to (A, not B). Monday 4-5-2026 is the correct weekday. |
