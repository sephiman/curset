## Notes (no effect on verdicts)

- q068 option «De los departamentos ministeriales que carezcan de un órgano superior propio» describes a category that
  does not exist (every ministerio has the Ministro as órgano superior, art. 55.3 LRJSP). It is still wrong by art. 6.1
  (ministries use the orden of their titular), so it is not failed, but it is the weakest distractor.
- q077: the window ends on 1-3-2027, Día de les Illes Balears (regional; art. 30.6 LPAC would make it inhábil if the
  interesado lived there). The stem's «registro de un ministerio» does not place anyone there, and the explanation's
  «no hay festivos» holds for national ones. Same note as q033.
- q075, q077, q079: arts. 12.3, 12.4 and 12.6 RD 1112/2018 are not basic legislation (STC 100/2019), so they bind the
  AGE. q077 places the request in a ministry; q075 and q079 name no entity, but cite the article. No effect on keys.
- q082 explanation's «La Red 060 nació del Acuerdo de 2005» names the Acuerdo without its date; correct per the
  preámbulo of Orden HAP/1949/2014.
- No lesson sentence found at odds with the norm in the passages these questions use. The lesson's art. 4.2 RD 127/2015
  sentence is faithful; it simply does not say that the DUE also carries the cese trámites (disp. adic. 3ª TRLSC).
