## m00-l01 — Cómo es el examen

Mapped against Anexo I (normas del ejercicio), norma común 11.1 and requirement R4.1 (C2 R4.1 takes C1 section 4,
whose R4.1 asks for «rules of R3.2, timing strategy as fact only, how the raw score is transformed, what the cut-off
meant in the last convocatoria from the published minimums»). The CPS notes are the three copied at the end of
`programa.md`: criterios de corrección (18-05-2026), puntuaciones mínimas (15-10-2026), fórmula de transformación
(29-09-2026).

Reference points:

- A1 Anexo I punto 1: 1.700 plazas, 156 reserved for disability (RD 651/2025).
- A2 Anexo I punto 2: «un ejercicio único, que constará de las dos partes […] ambas obligatorias y eliminatorias, y
  que se realizarán conjuntamente».
- A2.1 Anexo I 2.1: part 1, up to 60 questions (30 bloque I, 30 psicotécnico), 5 reserve valued in order.
- A2.2 Anexo I 2.2: part 2, up to 50 questions on bloque II, 5 reserve; Windows 11 and Microsoft 365 desktop;
  «noventa minutos»; alternative answers, one correct; same value; wrong minus one third; blank no penalty; invalid
  marks count as unanswered.
- A2.3.1 Anexo I 2.3.1: each part 0–50, minimum 25; the CPS fixes the minimum raw score of part 1 (to evaluate part 2)
  and of part 2.
- A2.3.2 Anexo I 2.3.2: transformation; minimum raw scores = minimum marks, not below 30 %; maxima = maximum marks;
  criteria published before the exam.
- A2.3.3 Anexo I 2.3.3: final mark = the exercise's mark; tie-breaks (five criteria, then orden de actuación 8.1/8.2).
- N11.1 Norma común 11.1: legislation in force on the date of each exercise.
- R4.1a rules of R3.2; R4.1b timing strategy as fact only; R4.1c how the raw score is transformed; R4.1d what the
  cut-off meant in the last convocatoria, from the published minimums.
