## Notes (no verdict changes)

1. q232: art. 16 RD 33/1986 caps the suspensión for a falta grave at three years («Si se impone por falta grave, no
   excederá de tres años»). The «tres años» distractor is still wrong, because the stem asks for the overall cap in
   art. 90.2. The explanation says only that three years «no aparecen en el artículo».
2. q253: the explanation calls the miércoles 20-01-2027 (third día hábil after publication) «el plazo de quien no cambia
   de residencia». Under art. 48.1 RD 364/1995 that three-día-hábil plazo runs from the day after the cese, not from
   publication. The wording is loose but names the source of the distractor correctly, and the count is right.
3. Lesson: no sentence was found at odds with the norm. The lesson gives the six-month rule of art. 90.1 TREBEP without
   the qualifications in art. 98.3 TREBEP (a provisional suspension in a judicial procedure longer than six months does
   not cost the puesto) and in art. 16 RD 33/1986 (a firme suspension no longer than the provisional one does not
   necessarily cost it). This is an omission, not a contradiction.
