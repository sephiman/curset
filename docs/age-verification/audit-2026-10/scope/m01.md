## m00-l01 — Cómo es el examen

Mapped against Anexo III (normas del ejercicio), norma común 11.1 and requirement R4.1 («rules of R3.2, timing
strategy as fact only, how the raw score is transformed, what the cut-off meant in the last convocatoria from the
published minimums»). The CPS notes are the files `2026-05-19_conv2025_criterios-correccion`,
`2026_conv2025_formula-transformacion` and `2026_conv2025_puntuaciones-minimas`.

Reference points:

- A1 Anexo III punto 1: plazas (2.512, 230 reserved for disability).
- A2 Anexo III punto 2: one exercise, two compulsory eliminatory parts taken together; «El tiempo máximo de realización
  de este ejercicio será de cien minutos».
- A2.1 Anexo III 2.1: part 1, up to 70 questions (40 bloques I–V, 30 bloque VI), 5 reserve; Windows 11 and
  Microsoft 365 desktop.
- A2.2 Anexo III 2.2: part 2, one case chosen from two, 20 questions plus 5 reserve; alternative answers, one correct;
  wrong answer minus one third; blank no penalty; part 2 not graded without a valid case mark; invalid marks count as
  unanswered.
- A2.3.1 Anexo III 2.3.1: each part 0–50, minimum 25; the CPS fixes the minimum raw score of each part.
- A2.3.2 Anexo III 2.3.2: transformation; minimum raw scores not below 30 %; criteria published before the exam.
- A2.3.3 Anexo III 2.3.3: final mark and tie-breaks.
- N11.1 Norma común 11.1: legislation in force on the exam date.
- R4.1a rules of R3.2; R4.1b timing strategy as fact only; R4.1c how the raw score is transformed; R4.1d what the
  cut-off meant in the last convocatoria, from the published minimums.
