## Notes (no verdict changed)

- q034: art. 22.3.c) RD 829/2023 only lists the Secretaría de Estado de Función Pública among the Ministry's organs; it
  does not say that it holds the old Secretaría de Estado para la Administración Pública's powers. The lesson (art. 54.1
  paragraph) rests the «hoy» on the same article. The explanation's «el Subsecretario del Departamento de origen … es
  justo quien ha informado en contra» is an inference: art. 54.1 says the informe is the Department's, not who signs it.
- Lesson, «Formas de provisión» and q007: art. 27.1 RD 364/1995 also covers puestos «transitoriamente» through interinos;
  the lesson does not mention it, and no sentence is wrong.
- Ley 30/1984 art. 20.1.c) (kept in force by the BOE note) allows concursos restricted to «áreas, sectores o
  departamentos» by authorisation of the Administración, without the Plan de Empleo that art. 41.1 RD 364/1995 requires.
  q012 names art. 41.1, so it stands; a no-norm stem on this point would not.
