# Exam style guide — Auxiliar Administrativo del Estado (C2), ingreso libre

Round R0a, 2026-11-05. Derived from the six distinct Auxiliar questionnaires: 2025 (23-05-2026, modelo A; modelo B repeats it in another order) as the primary reference; 2024 ordinario (modelo A) and extraordinario, 2022, and OEP 2020 ordinario and extraordinario (files `conv2020_*`) as secondary. There are no Auxiliar extraordinarios for 2025 or 2022. Every count comes from the per-question classification of the round (`docs/age-c2-official-exams-ledger.md`); text measures (lengths, negatives, keys) are counted from the questionnaire text.

The guide covers the knowledge questions: bloque I (part 1, questions 1–30 and the bloque I reserves) and bloque II (part 2, 50 + 5 reserves). Bloque II is described in two parts: temas II.1–II.4 (atención al público, información administrativa, registro y archivo, administración electrónica), which behave like bloque I, and temas II.5–II.12 (informática and ofimática), which have their own forms. The 30 psicotécnico questions of part 1 are generated, not authored; their style is in `docs/age-c2-psicotecnico-index.md` (section 11 here only points to it). It is not C1's guide: the Auxiliar exams have their own forms and lengths, and where they differ from C1 this guide says so.

## 1. Format

- One exercise of 90 minutes, two parts sat together (Anexo I 2). Part 1: 60 questions, 30 on bloque I and 30 psicotécnicas, plus 5 reserves. Part 2: 50 questions on bloque II, plus 5 reserves. Every questionnaire of 2020–2025 has exactly 60 + 5 and 50 + 5.
- Four options a)–d), exactly one correct. No question has more or fewer options. No combined options («a y b son correctas»).
- Order in part 1: bloque I questions 1–30 follow the programme order roughly (Constitución first, then Gobierno, AGE, UE, procedimiento, personal, presupuesto, igualdad); psicotécnicos 31–60. The part-1 reserves mix bloque I and psicotécnico questions (2025: two bloque I, three psicotécnico).
- Order in part 2: since 2022, questions 1–10 (2024: 1–11) are temas II.1–II.4 and 11–50 are ofimática in programme order: informática básica, Windows, Explorador, Word, Excel, Access, Outlook, Internet. OEP 2020 mixed them (2020 extr.: II.1–II.4 at 1 and 20–24). The part-2 reserves mix both.
- Penalty: a wrong answer costs one third of a correct one; blank costs nothing (Anexo I 2.2). Part 1 is scored on 60, part 2 on 50, each transformed to 0–50 (CPS notes, `sources/programa.md`).
- Typography: the stem is a full sentence ending in «:» (to complete) or a question «¿…?». Options start with a capital letter and usually end with a full stop; numeric options often have no full stop («41,40 €», «300»).

## 2. Stem forms

**Bloque I.** The dominant form names the norm and the article and asks to complete the sentence: «Conforme al artículo 167.1 de la Constitución Española de 1978, los proyectos de reforma constitucional deberán ser aprobados por:». In 2025, 13 of 32 bloque I questions (41 %) have that form, and 22 of 32 name an article. The norm is given with its full BOE title on first mention in the stem («Ley 40/2015, de 1 de octubre, de Régimen Jurídico del Sector Público»); OEP 2020 used acronyms after a first mention inside the questionnaire («CE», «LGP», «TREBEP»), 2022–2025 do not. Other forms: «¿Cuál de las siguientes…?» (2025: 22 %), «Señale la afirmación correcta sobre…, según…» (12 %), a norm named without an article (6–28 % by exam), and a few direct questions on facts without a norm (the year a State joined the EU, the organ for the Agenda 2030).

Example (2025, part 1, question 3; key d):

> Conforme al artículo 167.1 de la Constitución Española de 1978, los proyectos de reforma constitucional deberán ser aprobados por:
> a) Mayoría absoluta de cada una de las Cámaras.
> b) Una mayoría de dos tercios de ambas Cámaras.
> c) Una mayoría de dos tercios del Senado y mayoría absoluta del Congreso.
> d) Una mayoría de tres quintos de cada una de las Cámaras.

**Bloque II, temas II.1–II.4.** The norm is named without an article more often (2025: 7 of 12) and the stem is long, because it copies the norm's title and a whole definition: mean stem length 38–52 words against 25–36 in bloque I. Example (2025, part 2, question 1; key d):

> La función de atención personalizada al ciudadano relativa a facilitarle la orientación y ayuda que precisen en el momento inicial de su visita se denomina, según el Real Decreto 208/1996, de 9 de febrero, por el que se regulan los servicios de información administrativa y atención al ciudadano:
> a) De gestión y recepción.
> b) De orientación e información.
> c) De asistencia y orientación.
> d) De recepción y acogida.

**Ofimática (II.5–II.12).** Questions, not sentences to complete: «¿Cuál de las siguientes…?» (23–67 % by exam) and direct questions «¿Qué…? / ¿En qué situación…?» (29–56 %). The 2022 exam is the exception, with 13 sentences to complete. The stem names the application and its version in three quarters of the questions («En Microsoft Word 365 configurado en español», «En Windows 11», «En una hoja de Microsoft Excel 365»). 2025 stems often describe the path the user follows before asking («al hacer clic en el botón Inicio, seleccionar Configuración, entrar en el apartado Sistema y acceder a la sección Almacenamiento, ¿cuáles son…?»).

## 3. What the options are

**Bloque I.** Statements taken from the norm (19–48 % by exam), organs (9–25 %), quantities and majorities (3–16 %), plazos (3–12 %), procedures (0–9 %), and «otro»: named things that are not organs, such as States, sanctions, principles, rights (21–38 %). Dates are rare (0–1 per exam). No option is an article number. The four options share one grammatical frame and differ in the key element: «Mayoría absoluta de cada una de las Cámaras / Una mayoría de dos tercios de ambas Cámaras / …».

**II.1–II.4.** Mostly statements (38–78 %), the rest names of functions, services and offices (Cl@ve, DEHú, Red SARA, Punto de Acceso General). Option length varies widely by exam: 3–4 words (2024) to 16–17 (2022, 2024 extr.).

**Ofimática.** Statements describing a behaviour or a result (11–56 %), names of commands, tools, functions, file types or protocols (the «otro» row, 26–50 %), menu paths (2–14 %), function names (0–9 %), shortcuts (0–9 %), numbers (bits, characters, limits; 0–9 %). In 2025 half of the ofimática options are full sentences of 11 words on average; in 2022 and 2024 extr. most are one to three words.

## 4. «Ninguna de las anteriores» / «Todas son correctas»

Not used in knowledge questions. One ofimática question in 2025 has a «todas» option, not keyed. One psicotécnico of 2024 extr. has «Ninguna» and «Todas» options. The bank does not use them.

## 5. Negative stems

**Bloque I:** 0–2 per exam (2025: 2 of 32): «Señale cuál de los siguientes NO es un elemento del Municipio, según lo dispuesto en el artículo 11 de la Ley 7/1985…», «¿Cuál de las siguientes NO es una sanción disciplinaria, de conformidad con el artículo 96 del Real Decreto Legislativo 5/2015…?». The negative word is in capitals: NO, INCORRECTA.

**II.1–II.4:** 0–3 per exam.

**Ofimática:** frequent in OEP 2020 (10 and 8 per exam: «¿Cuál de las siguientes opciones NO…?»), rare since 2022 (2025: 1 of 43). Read 2025 as the target: at most one negative ofimática question per exam.

## 6. How distractors are built

**Bloque I.** The dominant pattern is the sibling figure: the other organs, majorities, sanctions or principles of the same article or list (2025: 62 %; other years 27–50 %). Then the norm's sentence altered (a word swapped: «integridad territorial y el ordenamiento democrático» → «integridad patrimonial y el ordenamiento constitucional»; 6–42 %), mixed sets (0–27 %), another plazo of the same norm (3–12 %), the organ one level up or down (0–9 %), a nearby number (0–12 %). Pure invention is rare (0–2 per exam). Distractors are wrong by the cited text, never merely unusual.

**II.1–II.4.** Sibling figures (33–78 %) and invented names of services or functions (9–33 %): «Dirección Electrónica Habilitada para usuarios / universal / única».

**Ofimática.** Other features of the same application (28–39 %), another application's command or a command of the same application that does something else (11–26 %), invention (11–35 %; it was the main pattern in OEP 2020, with made-up commands and behaviours), the real behaviour with one element changed (2–15 %), nearby numbers (0–16 %). 2025 distractors are long, plausible-sounding sentences that contain one false claim («…lo cual permite borrar el rastro del mensaje en el servidor del destinatario sin excepciones durante los primeros 30 segundos»).

The keyed option is the longest of the four in 3–25 % of bloque I questions and 12–24 % of ofimática questions. 2025 ofimática sits at the top (24 %). The bank keeps it at or below a quarter.

Keys are spread over the four letters in every exam (bloque I 2025: a 9, b 3, c 9, d 11). The platform shuffles options; the authored key letter does not matter.

## 8. Questions per tema

The full table is in «Statistics». Its totals cover 2022–2025: the OEP 2020 sets are «programa anterior» (as in C1, R3.4) and are shown but not counted. Bloque I: 30 questions over 16 temas, so most temas get one to three questions per exam. I.11 (LPAC, LRJSP, LJCA) is the heaviest (15 questions over four exams), then I.8, I.5 and I.14 (10–11); I.12 (protección de datos) and I.6 (Gobierno Abierto, Agenda 2030) are the lightest (3 and 5). Bloque II: temas II.1–II.4 take 11–13 of the 55 part-2 questions per exam (2025: 12); ofimática takes the rest, 42–44 (2025: 43). Among the ofimática temas, II.9 (Excel), II.12 (Internet), II.7 (Explorador) and II.5 (informática básica) get 23–26 questions over four exams; II.8 (Word), II.10 (Access) and II.11 (Outlook) 20–21; II.6 (Windows, escritorio, Copilot) 14, 13 of them since 2024.

## 9. Ofimática (bloque II, II.5–II.12)

The full index is `docs/age-c2-ofimatica-index.md`. Style points:

- Versions. Anexo I 2.2 fixes Windows 11 and Microsoft 365 desktop. 2025 stems say «Windows 11», «Microsoft Word 365», «Microsoft Excel 365», «Microsoft Outlook 365». 2022 and OEP 2020 stems say Windows 10 and Excel 2019. The bank uses the 2025 names only.
- Spanish locale. Stems say so when it matters («Microsoft Word 365 configurado en español») and options use the Spanish labels and function names exactly (SUMAR.SI.CONJUNTO, BUSCARX, DIASEM, «Recuperar este mensaje», «Retrasar entrega»). Shortcuts are those of the Spanish keyboard: in Word, CTRL + B opens the Navigation pane to search (2025, 29), CTRL + G saves.
- Forms: concept («¿Qué es…?»; the majority, 139 of 268), behaviour («¿qué ocurre si…?», «¿En qué situación… mostraría…?»), menu or settings path, configuration, function to use, formula result, shortcut. Formula items give the formula in the stem with Spanish separators («=SUMAR.SI.CONJUNTO(C2:C6;B2:B6;"Nómina";…)») and describe the result in words, not as a number, in 2025.
- Example (2025, part 2, question 32 (modelo B 33); key d):

  > En una hoja de Microsoft Excel 365, donde la Columna A es Fecha, Columna B es Concepto, Columna C es Importe, ¿qué valores se sumarán si se utiliza la siguiente fórmula? =SUMAR.SI.CONJUNTO(C2:C6;B2:B6;"Nómina";C2:C6;">2000";A2:A6;">=01/01/2025";A2:A6;"<=31/12/2025")
  > a) Sumará los importes mayores a 2.000 € del año 2025, siempre que la celda de concepto contenga el texto “Nómina”, aunque no coincida exactamente.
  > b) Sumará los importes de nómina del año 2025 y, además, los importes superiores a 2.000 € de otros conceptos distintos de nómina.
  > c) No devolverá ningún resultado, ya que el rango de suma y uno de los rangos de criterio son el mismo, lo cual provoca un error.
  > d) Sumará los importes correspondientes a nóminas con importe superior a 2.000 € y cuyas fechas estén comprendidas en el año 2025.

- II.5 (informática básica) and II.12 (Internet) are concept questions without an application: hardware, units, number systems, operating systems, security, protocols (IP, DNS, DHCP, FTP, ICMP), services and browsers. They name no version.
- Outlook. Classic and new Outlook differ; the official keys hold for classic Outlook. A bank question on a feature that differs says which Outlook it is about.
- Avoid what the official exams got wrong (ledger): keys that rest on Windows 10 labels (Centro de actividades, Herramientas administrativas), features retired since (WordPad, Asistente de concentración, Reconocimiento de voz de Windows, Visor 3D, «Presentar en línea»), and questions dated by «la última versión» (2022, 13, Android).

## 11. Psicotécnicos

Part 1, questions 31–60, are psicotécnicos. Their types, weights, parameters and one official example per type are in `docs/age-c2-psicotecnico-index.md`. They are generated by the platform (R6.2), not authored, so the checklist below does not apply to them. Their generators follow that index.

## 12. Reviewer checklist

Apply to every authored question of bloque I and bloque II. A question that fails any item is dropped (R5.4 of C1, by R5.2 of C2), not fixed in place.

All questions:

1. Four options, a)–d), exactly one correct by the cited text or the cited page. No other option is defensible under the same text, under another norm in force, or in another current version of the application.
2. The stem is a sentence to complete ending in «:» or a question «¿…?», in one of the forms of section 2 for its set.
3. No option is an article number, «Ninguna de las anteriores», «Todas son correctas» or a combination of letters.
4. The four options share grammatical form and register. None is absurd, a joke, or wrong on spelling alone.
5. The key is not systematically the longest option: across a tema's questions it is the longest in no more than about a quarter.
6. A negative stem has its negative word in capitals (NO, INCORRECTA) and there is no more than one per 20 questions of a tema (ofimática: no more than one per application per 20).
7. The question is not an official question copied or lightly reworded. Compare with `sources/examenes-oficiales/` and section 10.
8. Spanish is Peninsular and correct, with no typos, missing accents or stray characters. The official exams have some (ledger: «Errata»), and the bank does not copy them.
9. Citation, explanation, difficulty and tags are present (R5.3 of C1). The explanation says why the key is right and why each distractor is wrong.

Bloque I and temas II.1–II.4:

10. The stem names the norm with its BOE title and, where the question rests on one article, the article: «Conforme al artículo 167.1 de la Constitución Española de 1978, …», «Según el Real Decreto 208/1996, de 9 de febrero, por el que se regulan los servicios de información administrativa y atención al ciudadano, …». No acronym for the norm in the stem.
11. The key is the norm's wording or a faithful reduction of it. Every number, plazo, majority and organ matches the consolidated text named in `sources/normativa.yaml`, in the version in force on the exam date (cambio-pendiente rule, R3.5).
12. Distractors follow one or two patterns of section 6: a sibling figure of the same article or list, the norm's sentence with one element changed, another plazo or nearby number of the same norm, the organ one level off. Each is wrong by the cited text.
13. Organ and ministry names are the current ones (RD 829/2023, RD 1009/2023, RD 210/2024), unless the question is about a dated fact and says so.
14. Length: stem 20–40 words in bloque I and up to 55 in II.1–II.4 when it quotes a definition; options 3–20 words, statements up to 40 when the norm's sentence needs it.
15. No question on a «plan vigente», «última versión» or other fact that expires without a change in the law, unless the stem dates it («el IV Plan de Gobierno Abierto 2020-2024»).

Ofimática (II.5–II.12):

16. The stem names the application and version as 2025 does: «Windows 11», «Microsoft Word 365», «Microsoft Excel 365», «Microsoft Access 365», «Microsoft Outlook 365»; «configurado en español» when a shortcut or label depends on the locale. II.5 and II.12 concept questions name no application.
17. The feature exists in Windows 11 / Microsoft 365 desktop on the date of the round, and labels, paths, function names and shortcuts are the Spanish-locale ones. Nothing that exists only in Windows 10, Word para la web or one of the two Outlooks, unless the stem says which.
18. The key is supported by a saved Microsoft page in `sources/otros/` or, for a Spanish-locale label or shortcut the page does not give, checked on a Spanish-locale installation. A page that lists US-keyboard shortcuts (`ms-word-atajos.html`) is not a source for Spanish shortcuts.
19. Distractors are real commands, features or behaviours of the same application that do something else, or the real behaviour with one element changed. Invented command names are allowed only if they look like real ones and the key is unambiguous.
20. Formula questions use Spanish function names and «;» as separator, and the result follows from the stated data without assumptions the stem does not give.
21. Length: stem 15–35 words (up to 50 when it states a path or a formula); options as 2025: one to three words for names, one sentence of 8–20 words for behaviours.
