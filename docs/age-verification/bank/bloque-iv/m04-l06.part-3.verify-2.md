## Notes (no verdict changed)

1. **Lesson, «Resolución» (m04-l06.md).** It lists the Consejo de Ministros as competent for separación (art. 47.1 RD 33/1986) without noting LRJSP art. 61.s (Ministers impose separación por faltas muy graves). The lesson is incomplete against a norm in force; the bank ledger item 9 is still open.
2. **LPAC 82.2 and the PA plazos.** LPAC 82.2 gives 10–15 días for alegaciones; LPAC is outside the bloque IV reading set and RD 33/1986 arts. 36, 41 and 43 are the special rules for these trámites, so q236 (quince días), q240 (26 April, 15 hábiles) and q245 are passed on that basis. The coordinator may want this ruled explicitly.
3. **Regional festivos.** q241 (9 Oct 2026, Comunitat Valenciana, a Friday in the window) and q250 (9 Nov 2027, Comunitat Valenciana and Madrid city, a Tuesday in the window) are outside the stems' «único festivo». Passed because the stems state the holidays; the counts change if a regional calendar applies.
4. **Explanations, wording.** q247 («El lunes 15 cuenta como primero el día de la notificación») and the same construction in q238, q239, q240 and q254 mean that the distractor date results when the notification day is counted as day one. The sense is right but the phrasing is elliptical; q251 paraphrases «no superior a un mes» as «en el mes siguiente».
5. **q231 citation field.** Lists only RD 365/1995 art. 21.4; the key also rests on TREBEP 98.3.
6. **q255 and LRJSP 61.s.** Passed on the confinement to RD 33/1986 art. 47.1 in the stem. If the stem were to name only «la normativa» it would fail check 2.
7. **Key position.** No key in the batch is strictly the longest option (checklist 9).
