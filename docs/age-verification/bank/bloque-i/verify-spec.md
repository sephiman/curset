## Per question

named in `sources/normativa.yaml`, later version where `cambios_pendientes` applies) or the cited saved page in
`sources/otros/`. Do not trust the draft's `source_quote`; find the text yourself. Answer five checks:

1. **Key.** Is the keyed option correct by the cited text?
2. **Unique.** Is exactly one option correct? A distractor that is defensible under the cited text or another norm in
   force, or true in a reading a candidate could reasonably take, fails.
3. **Citation.** Does the cited norm + article (+ apartado) contain the answer? Is the lesson citing that same article
   (search the lesson for it)?
4. **Style.** Checklist § 12 items 1–11, 16, 17: stem form and «:»/«¿…?»; norm named by title; no article-number option,
   no «ninguna/todas»/combined option; negative stem only in capitals; correct option is the norm's wording or a
   faithful reduction; distractors from one or two § 6 patterns, each wrong by the text; options share form, none
   absurd; lengths (stem 15–40 words, options 5–20, statements to 40); current organ names; Peninsular Spanish with no
   typos; explanation says why the key is right and why each distractor is wrong, and is itself correct.
5. **Not official.** The question is not an official question (in `official/I.TT.md`) copied or lightly reworded: same
   stem with the same option set, or the same options re-ordered, fails. Testing the same fact from another angle passes.
