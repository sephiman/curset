## Detail

- **q072, key and citation.** Orden HAC/557/2026 art. 6.1.1: «Los programas finalistas son aquellos a los que se puede
  asignar objetivos cuantificables e indicadores de ejecución mensurables». Key c is that text. The lesson cites the same
  article (§ «Programas finalistas e instrumentales», and «Lo que más cae»). Options a (centro gestor único: LGP 35.2
  puts every program under one centro gestor, so it does not separate the classes), b (ingresos afectados: LGP 27.3
  affects resources only by law, not programs) and d (operaciones de capital: no norm uses it) are wrong by the text.
- **q072, unique.** Passes on the Orden and the LGP; the failure is check 5 only.
- **q072, style.** Stem 39 words (limit 40). Options 12–14, key not the longest (a is). Explanation names options by
  content and is correct.
- **q073, key and citation.** Art. 6.1.1 again. The key paraphrases «indicadores de ejecución mensurables» as
  «indicadores mensurables»: a faithful reduction. Lesson cites the same article.
- **q073, unique.** Distractor b («unidades físicas») is wrong: the Orden says objectives are expressed in physical units
  «cuando ello sea posible» for finalistas, not as a mark of instrumentales. Distractor d («apoyo a un programa finalista»)
  is a purpose the Orden lists for instrumentales, but the Orden's first sentence fixes finalista by the objectives test,
  so d is wrong on the Orden; not a fail on its own. Distractor a is the failing one (see table).
- **q073, check 5.** The official conv2022_X P1 17 states the definition; q073 applies it to a program described by its
  features (angle changed) and carries no official option. Passes under «testing the same fact from another angle».
- **q073, style.** Stem 40 words (limit). Options 10–14. Explanation names options by content; its claim that «la Orden
  no establece» the multi-year condition is true of the Orden and is exactly why the option cannot be keyed false
  without reading LGP 35.2 out.

## Notes (do not change a verdict)

  distractor a defensible. Either the lesson wording or the bank needs to say that the Orden's test is the objectives
  test, not the time span.
