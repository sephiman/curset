## Notes (no verdict change)

- q273, option «Comporta la inhabilitación para un nuevo nombramiento como interino»: art. 56.1.d) TREBEP bars a
  separated person from selection «para el acceso al cuerpo o escala de funcionario … en el que hubiese sido separado».
  That is a selection requirement for one cuerpo, not a general inhabilitation carried by art. 96.1.a), so the option
  stays wrong as worded; flagged because a candidate could connect the two.
- q271 speaks of incoating «el expediente» for a falta leve; art. 18.2 RD 33/1986 does not require an expediente for
  leves (only audiencia). The prescription point the question tests is unaffected.
- q273, q279, q280 explanations refer to «la letra c)», «la letra j)» etc. These are letters of the article, named with
  the article, not option letters.
- Lesson: line 290 states the art. 94.3 TREBEP rule (suspension on «indicios fundados de criminalidad») without the
  different rule of art. 23 RD 33/1986 (the instructor reports to the Ministerio Fiscal and the expediente continues,
  except for delitos of títulos II and VII of libro segundo of the Código Penal, which suspend it). The ledger's IV.6
  list of TREBEP / RD 33/1986 differences does not record it either. Not at odds with the norm the lesson cites, but a
  «which rule applies» gap.
