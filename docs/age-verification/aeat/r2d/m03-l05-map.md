## 1. «Información y Asistencia a los obligados tributarios.»
Governing: LGT arts. 85–91 (sección 2.ª, with arts. 88–89 consultas tributarias escritas); RGAT arts. 62–78 (sección 1.ª
of capítulo II, título III).

Decision on consultas (arts. 88–89 LGT; R2b removed them): inside. They sit in the sección whose heading the programme
copies verbatim («Información y asistencia a los obligados tributarios»), art. 85.2.c lists «Contestaciones a consultas
escritas» among the actuaciones of that duty, and the lesson itself prints 85.2.c (l. 13). The 2025 programme dropping
the words «la consulta tributaria» does not take an article out of the sección it names; R4.7 supersedes the R2b
«unmapped and unasked → cut» reading. The two old questions (2021-46, 2021-47) were ruled `fuera de temario` on the same
wording ground; they become reference evidence again.

## R2b removals inside this tema
- «Consultas tributarias escritas», «Efectos de las contestaciones a consultas» (arts. 88–89 LGT): **inside** sentence 1.
  The programme sentence is the verbatim heading of LGT sección 2.ª, which contains arts. 88–89, and art. 85.2.c (kept,
