## Lesson/norm disagreements and gaps

1. **Art. 29.2.d (sigilo) is not in the lesson.** The conv2020 ordinario question 5 keys «No guardar el debido sigilo
   respecto a los asuntos que se conozcan por razón del cargo…» as grave. The lesson lists only the abuso de autoridad
   and the abstención case under 29.2, so the official's exact fact cannot be asked. q045 covers the classification with
   a fact the lesson states. Suggest adding 29.2.d to the lesson's list of graves.
2. **Art. 2.1, minor wording.** The lesson's sentence ends «…las Universidades públicas y las corporaciones de Derecho
   Público en sus actividades sujetas a Derecho Administrativo». In the norm the qualifier belongs to the corporaciones
   (letra e) only, and letra b) also includes the mutuas colaboradoras. Not wrong, but the sentence can be read as
   qualifying the whole list. No question rests on it.
3. **Typo in the norm text.** Art. 17.3 reads «por si sola» (no accent) in the BOE file. The `source_quote` of q021
   keeps it; the option writes «por sí sola».
4. The art. 19.3 end of suspension and DA 4.ª.3, amended in the lesson today, match the norm (q030, q042).

No other disagreement found. The «hoy» ministry notes were not needed: q010 asks the Portal's dependence «según el
texto legal» and the key quotes the law («Ministerio de la Presidencia»); the Ministerio de Hacienda y Administraciones
Públicas appears in quotes only. Current ministry names in options follow RD 829/2023.
