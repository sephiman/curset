## Lesson / norm disagreements and risks

1. **Saved-page citations may not resolve.** The lesson cites the head-count pages as «(sources/otros/ue-parlamento-europeo.html)»,
   not in the `fuente:` form the citation grammar reads (`citations/grammar.py`, `LEAD` needs `fuente:`). The four
   questions on those pages (q023, q034, q043, q045) carry `fuente: \`file.html\`` as the spec says. If the lesson keeps
   its current form, the load may reject them because the lesson «never cites» that anchor. Fix in the lesson:
   «(fuente: `ue-parlamento-europeo.html`)» and the same for the other three, or drop the four questions.
2. **Which consolidated text.** The opening paragraph says the lesson follows the versions «publicadas en el Diario
   Oficial de la Unión Europea C 202, de 7 de junio de 2016». The «Normativa» block and `normativa.yaml` give the
   consolidated version of 15-03-2025. No article the lesson cites changed between the two (only Protocols 3 and 5),
   so no fact differs. The paragraph is out of date.
3. No fact in the lesson contradicts the norm. Omissions only: art. 258 TFUE leaves out that the Commission hears the
   State before issuing the dictamen motivado; art. 236 TFUE says the European Council's list covers formations
   «distintas de la de Asuntos Generales y la de Asuntos Exteriores» (the lesson says «las demás formaciones»). Same
   meaning in both.
4. q033 (17.5 TUE) keeps «un nacional de cada Estado miembro» out of the distractors. The Commission does have 27
   members, so a verifier could defend that option even though art. 17.5 gives the two-thirds rule.
