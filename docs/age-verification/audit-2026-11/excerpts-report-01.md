## Misses and flags by cause

**Future-wording articles: the «Redacción anterior» is not separated from the text in force.** The BOE label
lines («Téngase en cuenta…», «Redacción anterior:») are dropped, but the quoted old wording stays, so the unit
holds both versions with nothing to tell them apart.

- Excerpt picks the old wording instead of the one in force (the fact is in the current wording only in full):
  86 `m04-l09-q018` (169.1.a), 90 `m04-l09-q022` (172). Both are scored full-only. Paragraph selection lands on
  the quoted copy, probably because it matches the question's spelled-out numerals («ciento ochenta»). In both
  the learner sees a text that stops applying on 01-12-2026, cross-referenced by the old paragraph numbering.
- Excerpt shows the current wording but runs into the opening of the quoted old wording before «[…]»:
  91 `m04-l09-q023` (172), 93 `m04-l09-q026`, 94 `m04-l09-q027`, 95 `m04-l09-q028` (173.1).
- Full only (excerpt clean): 87, 88, 89 (169: old 1.a) block spliced between 1.a) and 1.b)), 92 (171: old text
  appended).

**Citation scope (not an extractor fault).** 83 `m04-l09-q015`: the key needs 44.2 (tributación), but the
question cites 44.1. The excerpt shows exactly 44.1; the fact is in full.

No garbled text, page headers/footers or «►» markers found in this batch. Every excerpt is the unit its
heading names.
