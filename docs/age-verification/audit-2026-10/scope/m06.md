# Scope audit R2b Part A — bloque VI (m06)

Date 2026-10-17. Read-only audit of `content/age-administrativo-c1/es/lessons/m06-l01.md` … `m06-l08.md` against
`sources/programa.md` (Anexo III, punto 6, bloque «VI. Informática básica y ofimática»), following
`scope/spec.md`. In bloque VI the governing «norm» is the feature set each programme sentence names (Anexo III 2.1:
Windows 11 and Microsoft 365 desktop). Exam evidence comes from `docs/age-ofimatica-index.md` (status column = ledger
estado), checked against the questionnaires in the session scratchpad (`exams/*_cuestionario.txt`). `conv2020*` was sat
in 2022 under the «programa anterior»; those questions are marked «(2020, programa anterior)».

Sentence splitting: at «. »; a sentence whose elements follow a colon or are separated by commas is split into
elements (S3a, S3b…). «X y Y» without a comma (e.g. «Herramientas "Este equipo" y "Acceso rápido"») is not split, and
the note says whether both halves are covered. A sentence whose colon introduces a single element («Procesadores de
texto: Word 365.») is not split.

Every lesson of the bloque is list-shaped: each `##` heading reproduces one programme sentence (or two), so every
section maps by construction. The «Note» column records bullets inside a mapped section that sit closer to another
sentence or another tema, and repeats across lessons; they are observations, not verdicts.
