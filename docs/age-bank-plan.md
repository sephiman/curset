## 3. Target mix per tema

### Stem forms

Within ±10 points of each 2025 part-1 share (style guide § 2). The 2025 part-1 shares include bloque VI, whose stems
never name an article, so bloques I–V shift weight from the direct question to the article-named forms. Two profiles:

| Form (style guide § 2) | 2025 part 1 | Profile N (normative temas) | Profile G (I.6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| «De acuerdo con / Según el artículo X de [norma], …:» (to complete) | 31 % | 38 % | 21 % |
| «Según el artículo X …, ¿…?» | 11 % | 15 % | 5 % |
| Direct question «¿…?», no norm named | 31 % | 22 % | 41 % |
| «¿Cuál de las siguientes…?» | 16 % | 14 % | 21 % |
| Statement to complete, no norm named | 6 % | 6 % | 6 % |
| «Señale la respuesta correcta…» (or «INCORRECTA») | 3 % | 3 % | 3 % |
| «Según la [norma]» without an article | 3 % | 2 % | 3 % |
| **Stem names the article** (first two rows) | **53 %** (79/150 named one) | **53 %** | **26 %** |

- Profile N applies to every tema of bloques I–V except I.6. Profile G applies to I.6, whose sources are mostly the
  Agenda 2030 resolution, the OECD Recommendation and the Portal de la Transparencia pages: they have paragraphs and
  sections, not articles, and the stem names «el apartado» or «el párrafo» where the source numbers it.
- A stem «names the article» when it carries «artículo N» (or «apartado N», «disposición …», «párrafo N» of a source
  without articles). Direct and «¿Cuál…?» stems may still name the norm without the article.
- The ±10 band is checked per tema after verification, on the kept questions.

## 4. Shape in the repository

- **Exercises.** `content/age-administrativo-c1/exercises/mBB-lTT-ex-N.yaml`, `type: quiz`, one variant per question,
  about 15–25 variants per exercise, grouped by the lesson's sections in lesson order. Each exercise is listed under its
  lesson in `course.yaml`. A random draw counts each variant as one question.
- **Question id** (variant id): `mBB-lTT-qNNN`, numbered per tema in authoring order, never reused. A dropped question's
  id is not given to its replacement.
- **Fields** (R5.3): `prompt`, four `options` (`a`–`d`, one `correct`), `explanation`, `citation` (as a lesson cites it,
  norm + article + paragraph: «art. 167.1 CE»), `difficulty`, `tags`. The lesson anchor the citation resolves to is
  computed with the platform's resolver at build time, written as a `# anchor:` comment above the variant, and checked
  by the load (a question whose citation its lesson never cites fails the load).
- **Working files.** `docs/age-verification/bank/bloque-<n>/`: the writer spec, per-tema drafts (`mBB-lTT.draft.json`),
  verification reports (`mBB-lTT.verify-<batch>.md`), the drop log and the built statistics.

## 5. Verification (R5.4)

Per batch of a tema, by an agent that did not write it, with the cited article open:

1. Is the key correct by the cited text?
2. Is exactly one option correct (no distractor defensible under that text or another norm in force)?
3. Does the citation contain the answer?
4. Does the question pass the style guide's checklist (§ 12)?
5. Is it not a verbatim or lightly reworded official question (compared with the tema's official questions)?

Any FAIL drops the question; it is never fixed in place. The writer may author a replacement under a new id, and the
replacement goes through the same check. Per tema the ledger records authored, dropped by reason, kept.
