## 1. Scenario

538 words. Every weekday is right (30 Jun; 17, 15, 16, 19, 29 Jul; 30, 31 Aug; 8, 14 Sep; 26, 27 Oct; 1, 4, 9, 16 Nov;
6, 8 Dec 2027). Real Madrid 2027 calendar (Decreto 82/2026 plus the city's local days): 1, 6 Jan; 19 Mar; 25, 26 Mar;
1 May; 15 May; 16 Aug (Asunción moved from Sunday); 12 Oct; 1, 9 Nov; 6, 8, 25 Dec. Every day-counted window (q04–q06,
q08, q18, q19) holds only the named festivos, or none. The month plazos end on hábiles, or on the named 6 Dec.

- **SCENARIO (fault).** «Conservas Riberas del Júcar, S.L. recurrió en alzada su reintegro de una subvención, y la
  persona titular del Ministerio lo desestimó expresamente» and «Hortalizas de la Mancha Alta, S.A. presenta … su
  alzada contra otro reintegro». Art. 42.5 LGS: «La resolución del procedimiento de reintegro pondrá fin a la vía
  administrativa». Art. 121.1 LPAC allows alzada only against acts that «no pongan fin a la vía administrativa». So
  neither alzada exists. The appeal is reposición, or a direct contencioso, and the FEGA's own notices on
  reintegro-type files say exactly that (e.g. BOE-B-2013-4489: Presidente del FEGA «desestima el Recurso de
  Reposición»; contencioso «ante el Juzgado Central … artículo 9 c)»).
- **SCENARIO (fault, forum).** «La empresa quiere acudir a la Sala de lo Contencioso-administrativo de la Audiencia
  Nacional.» This is the company's wish, but the case gives no forum for it. An act of the FEGA, an organismo público
  with national competence, goes to the Juzgados Centrales (art. 9.1.c LJCA). A ministerial decision on appeal goes
  to the Sala of the Audiencia Nacional only when it **rectifies** that act (art. 11.1.b: «cuando rectifiquen en vía de
  recurso … los dictados por órganos o entes distintos con competencia en todo el territorio nacional»). Here the
  Minister dismissed the appeal.
- **Effect on questions.** q01 (BOE anuncio) and q02 (two months from the express act, with August excluded) keep
  their answers: under reposición, art. 46.4 LJCA gives the same date. q03 and q04 change (table).
- **Note, not a fault.** q18 counts from the day the file is sent («remite»). Art. 13.2 counts from receipt. The
  reading «received the same day» is the natural one, and no option depends on a later receipt.
- **Consistent otherwise.** No renamed person, and no date comes after the act it precedes. The FEGA is an organismo
  autónomo adscrito al Ministerio de Agricultura, Pesca y Alimentación (current name). The 2028 and 2029 annuities can
  fit art. 47.2 LGP on a 90.000 credit.
- **Fiction and voice.** A web search found no company with the names Conservas Riberas del Júcar, Frutas del Jarama
  or Ganadería Sierra Norte. The other names are generic, and the people match no public figure I know of. Nothing
  from the «never does» list appears.
