| overview | FAIL | Every cited statement checks against art. 103 Ley 31/1990 (Uno.1, Uno.2, Tres.1 on rank and appointment of the Presidente and Director General). The sentence that lists the units has no citation and is wrong as written: «La organización central reparte el trabajo en departamentos, servicios centrales, Consejo Superior y Consejos Territoriales». The Consejo Superior para la Dirección y Coordinación de la Gestión Tributaria and the Consejos Territoriales are collegiate bodies with representatives of the State and of the Comunidades Autónomas, set up to coordinate the management of the tributos cedidos (arts. 65.1 and 66.1 Ley 22/2009). They are not units through which the AEAT's central organisation shares out its work. The Consejos Territoriales also act «en su respectivo ámbito territorial», so placing them in the central organisation misleads the reader. Orden de 2-6-1994, apartado primero, lists the central bodies as Departamentos, Servicios and the Delegación Central de Grandes Contribuyentes. |
