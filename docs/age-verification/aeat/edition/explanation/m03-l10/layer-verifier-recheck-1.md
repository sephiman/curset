| caso:### Compensación a instancia del obligado tributario#1 | FAIL | The facts contradict each other. «con un crédito que ya cumple los requisitos» and «La deuda se extingue el 12 de marzo (art. 72.3 LGT)» sit next to «hasta el reconocimiento del crédito, el martes 5 de mayo». Art. 72.3 dates extinction to the filing only when the requirements for debt and credit are already met; a credit not yet recognised (RGR art. 56.1.c requires a «crédito reconocido») meets them on recognition, so extinction would then be 5 May, not 12 March. If the credit did meet the requirements on 12 March, the debt was extinguished inside the voluntary period (which ends 20 April), and pinning interest «hasta … el martes 5 de mayo» implies interest runs in this case. Either way the reader comes away with a wrong picture of how art. 72.2 and art. 72.3 interact. Dates and weekdays are right (12 Mar Thu, 20 Apr Mon, 5 May Tue). |

Its debt is unpaid in the voluntary period with no credit to offset (art. 60.2.a).
