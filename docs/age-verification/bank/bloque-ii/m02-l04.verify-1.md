- Failing: q008 (unique), q026 (style).
- Checklist 9: the key is strictly the longest option in 4 of 33 (q011, q022, q026, q032). No negative stems. No
  explanation names an option by letter or position. Authored keys a/b/c/d = 12/6/6/9 (a is heavy in this batch).

Notes that do not change a verdict:

- q004, q010, q011, q012, q013, q014 name the norm as «Ley Orgánica 3/2018, de Protección de Datos Personales y garantía
  de los derechos digitales», without «de 5 de diciembre». Number and title identify it uniquely; passed. q017 and q028
  name «el Reglamento general de protección de datos», the norm's own short title. If the round reads checklist 2 as
  requiring the full BOE form, these are the ones affected.
- q013's explanation says the month runs «sin festivo en el intervalo». True for national festivos; lunes 1-3-2027 is
  the Día de les Illes Balears. It does not change a date-to-date month ending on a hábil Tuesday.
- q010 («mayor de dieciséis años») and q019 («en más de dos ocasiones durante el plazo de seis meses») are nearby-figure
  distractors that a literal reader could call entailed by the real threshold (a 17-year-old is over fourteen; three
  accesses are more than one). Read, as the official cifra-cercana questions are, as stating the threshold, they are
  wrong; passed. q008 fails because its narrowed options describe groups of persons the norm names, not a threshold.
- q011's stem says «un menor de quince años» to mean a fourteen-year-old; the key holds for any age under fifteen.
