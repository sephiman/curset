## Notes (no effect on verdicts)

1. q246 names «la Ley General Presupuestaria» and «la Ley 31/2022» without their full titles. This is accepted because
   the official exams do the same («Conforme al artículo 60 de la Ley General Presupuestaria», conv2024_X·P1·36).
   Art. 9.Ocho Ley 31/2022 also lets the titular authorize some capítulo 2 transfers between services (gastos comunes de
   edificios compartidos). The stem confines the question to 63.1.a) and 9.Siete, so the key holds.
2. The q265 key says the restrictions «no alcanzan a los traspasos entre programas de una sección». That is true for
   restrictions a) and b) on the stated facts. Restriction c), on extraordinary, supplemented or enlarged credits, could
   still apply to such a transfer.
3. Lesson, at odds with the norm: the citations in the esquema «Estructura de los estados de gastos» are shifted by one
   letter. Orgánica is cited as 40.1.b), programas as 40.1.c) and the estados de gastos header as 40.1.a). The prose
   correctly cites 40.1.a) for orgánica, 40.1.b) for programas and 40.1 for the header.
