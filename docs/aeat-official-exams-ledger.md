# Ledger de exámenes oficiales — Agentes de la Hacienda Pública, acceso libre (primer ejercicio)

Round R0a, 2026-11-11. Input for the coverage audit (requirements R4.6), for «Lo que más cae» (R4.2) and, in stage 2,
for the «Exámenes oficiales» practice set (R6.2). The set is not built here. Generated by
`docs/age-verification/aeat/r0a/scripts/gen_ledger.py`; edit the classification or `classification/overrides.json`, not
this file.

Columns: **Nº** as numbered in the tipo A questionnaire (the extraordinario of 2022 has only a tipo A). **Tipo B**: the
number of the same question in tipo B, matched by stem and options (`checks/pairing.json`). **Tema**: the 2025
programme (`sources/programa.md`), coded I.1–I.7, II.1–II.5, III.1–III.20; a second tema in brackets when the choice is
debatable, or, for a `fuera de temario` question, the nearest 2025 tema. **Estado**: see below. **Clave**: as printed on
the definitive answer sheet. **Nota de clave**: «Clave oficial discutida» and the reason. **Nota**: what the Tribunal
decided on the question (annulment, key changed between the provisional and the definitive sheet), errata in the
official text, and why a question is excluded or doubtful.

Estado (as C1's ledger, `docs/age-official-exams-ledger.md`):

- `vigente`: enters the practice set with the official key, no note.
- `vigente-con-nota`: enters the set with the official key and a visible note: «Clave oficial discutida» (the key is not
  supported by the norm text or is not unique) or «Denominación actual» (an organ renamed since).
- `norma modificada: <norma> art. X`: the answer changed with the law; excluded. «(cambio-pendiente, desde
  DD-MM-YYYY)» marks a change already published that takes effect later.
- `fuera de temario`: the topic is in no tema of the 2025 programme (it was in the programme of its own convocatoria);
  excluded.
- `dudosa`: could not be settled; excluded.
- `anulada`: voided on the definitive answer sheet; not in the set.

Status was checked against the consolidated texts on boe.es as of 2026-11-11 (`sources/normativa.yaml`). Official keys
are never a source of facts for authored questions: authored questions cite the norm text only.

**Programmes.** Every questionnaire is classified against the 2025 programme (32 temas). The earlier convocatorias had
programmes of 34–37 temas (`sources/programa.md`, «Cambios entre convocatorias»); a question on a topic the 2025
programme dropped is `fuera de temario`, whatever its own programme said. The coverage set of requirement R4.6 («the
last four convocatorias») is OEP 2022–2025 with the 2022 extraordinario; OEP 2020 and 2021 are kept for style and for
the practice set, outside the per-tema totals.

**Exam dates and models.** The 2020, 2021 and 2023 questionnaires print the exam date on the cover; the others do not, and
their dates come from the BOE resolutions that convened the exercise (handoff). Tipo B repeats tipo A in another order.
The definitive keys of tipo A and tipo B name the same option text for every pair (`checks/pairing.json`), so tipo B
rows are not repeated here.

**Answer sheets.** `checks/provisional-vs-definitive.txt` compares the provisional and definitive sheets of every exam.
The 2022 extraordinario has only a provisional sheet online; the Tribunal's resolution says it became definitive
«sin modificación alguna».
