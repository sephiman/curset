## Notes (no verdict change)

- The lesson cites «art. 34.1.b) LGP» for «Al ejercicio presupuestario, que coincide con el año natural, …». The
  «año natural» clause is in the lead-in of art. 34.1, not in letter b).
- q005, q026 and q030 name the IOC as «la Instrucción de operatoria contable» and leave out «a seguir en la ejecución
  del gasto del Estado». q005 also names the LGP as «la Ley General Presupuestaria». Both were accepted under the ruling
  for titles that would push the stem past 40 words, but the short IOC form is not one the official exams use.
- q030's stem has the «servicio gestor» authorise and commit the gasto. Under regla 21.1 and regla 22.1 IOC the
  competent authority does that, and the servicio gestor only issues the documents.
