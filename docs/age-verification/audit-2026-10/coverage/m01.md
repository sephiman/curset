## Partials and misses

- **m01-l01, 2025 extr. (conv2025_X), P1 19 (I.1 o I.5), miss.** Missing fact: the prestación del consentimiento del
  Estado by treaties that suppose the modification or repeal of a law or require legislative measures for their

  is in m01-l03 («Los tratados internacionales»), which is neither the owner nor the alternative; m01-l01 and m01-l05
  do not state it.
- **m01-l03, 2022 ord. (conv2022), P1 15 (I.3 o V.2), miss.** Missing fact: the Ley de Presupuestos cannot create
  taxes; it may modify them when a substantive tax law so provides. Article: CE art. 134.7. No lesson of the course
  cites art. 134.7 (m05-l01 covers art. 134.1–4 only; the alternative m05-l02 has none of art. 134).
