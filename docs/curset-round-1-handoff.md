## 2. R9.5 remainder

- **Frontend golden suites iterate over every published course.** `src/test/courseContent.ts` gained a
  per-course loader (`courseContent(dir)`, `publishedCourses(root)`, `GOLDEN_COURSES`). The
  glossary-links report, the lesson-refs report, the list-shape checks and the directive guard run once
  per course and per language it declares. Each course keeps its goldens in its own directory. ES/EN
  parity checks run only for two-language courses. The size floors stay pinned to crypto-futures by
  name. The crypto-only suites (PDF, Android bundle) keep their crypto-bound helpers, unchanged.
- **The proof course.** `GOLDEN_COURSES` = the published courses under `content/` (today
  crypto-futures) plus the published backend test fixtures (`fixture-oposiciones`, Spanish only, no
  figures, reusing crypto's ids). The fixture gained a second lesson, `m01-l2`, which mentions `m01-l1`
  and the glossary term. That gives its new goldens (`glossary-links.es.txt`, `lesson-refs.es.txt`)
  one real row each, resolved inside that course. The crypto goldens regenerated byte-identical.
- **"No figures" lines.**
  - Backend: `test_figure_prose_coupling.py` iterates over every pipeline course
    (`tests/pipeline_courses.py`). A course without figures has its own passing test, and `pytest` ends
    with a summary section:
    ```
    ============================ figure steps per course ============================
    crypto-futures: 34 figures
    fixture-oposiciones: no figures
    ```
  - PDF export: a course with no figures or no exercise charts skips that capture phase. It reports
    `<course>: no figures` / `<course>: no exercise charts` through the new `onSkipped` hook (console
    by default), never "0 of 0". A real typesetting test on the fixture course checks the phases are
    exactly `export → exercises → typeset`.
- **Generation goldens and fingerprints stay in `dist/contracts/`.** The README ("The Android bundle and
  the port's contracts") now says they describe the generators, not a course.
