| after:### Sanciones no pecuniarias por infracciones graves o muy graves#1 | FAIL | «Duran un año si la infracción es grave y dos si es muy grave» is stated as the general rule, but art. 186.2 LGT sets three, four or five years when the fine for a muy grave infracción is equal to or above 60.000, 150.000 or 300.000 euros (with comisión repetida). The reader would wrongly conclude that the accessory sanctions for a muy grave infracción always last two years, whatever the fine. The 30.000-euro threshold and the two-year term hold only within art. 186.1. |
