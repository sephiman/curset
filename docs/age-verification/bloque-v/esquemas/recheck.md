## Notes

1. m04-l02 rows 1.1–1.2 and the prose say «un cupo no inferior al cinco por ciento» without the base («de las vacantes»). The TREBEP row beside them has «de las vacantes» and the RDL 6/2023 row has «de las plazas convocadas». Nothing is misstated. If the author wants the three bases comparable, «de las vacantes» would have to go into the prose (line 154) first and then into both rows. This is optional and not a FAIL.
2. m05-l02 row 5.3 cites «art. 54.1–2 LGP». The prose cites «art. 54.1 LGP» twice and names «artículo 54.2» only in running text, with no citation of its own (line 232). The fact is in the prose and the reference is right. Strictly, the citation span 54.2 has no prose citation behind it. Acceptable. Optionally, line 232 could read «(art. 54.2 LGP)».
3. m05-l02 row 5.4 is a bare «Cuando…» clause under the header «En qué consiste». It is accurate, but it reads as the case rather than the definition. Prose line 187 opens «Proceden cuando…». Starting the cell with «Proceden cuando…» would match the prose. Style only.
4. m05-l04 row 7.3 (and m04-l02 row 1.1) carry no vigencia condition for Ley 30/1984. In the consolidated text, art. 23 Ley 30/1984 stays in force «en tanto no se oponga» to the TREBEP until the leyes de Función Pública are dictated (DF 4.ª.2 TREBEP, prose m05-l04 lines 24–29). That condition comes from another norm, and a row cites one norm. The TREBEP row's label (once fixed per 7.2) shows the boundary. No change proposed.
5. m05-l06 step 2 leaves out the last sentence of art. 70.3 RLGS: presenting in the extra fifteen days «no eximirá al beneficiario de las sanciones». The prose (lines 150–152) leaves it out too. It is a consequence, not a condition of the plazo. Step 1 does not mention that art. 70.1 RLGS allows the órgano concedente to extend the justification plazo by up to half, and the prose does not mention it either. Both gaps sit in articles other than the cited 30.2 LGS, so neither makes the rows wider than their article. They are listed here for the ledger.
