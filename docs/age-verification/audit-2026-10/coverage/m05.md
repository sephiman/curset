pagos a justificar. m05-l03 states those facts, in its table and in «Lo que más cae» (arts. 2.2, 2.3 RD 725/1989; art.
10.1 RD 640/1987; art. 79.5 LGP). m05-l05 itself only points there («Sus límites, la rendición de cuentas y la
reposición de fondos están en m05-l03»). The ledger puts these rows in V.5 with no V.3 alternative, so they count
against m05-l05. The course states the facts, but in the lesson of the other tema.
