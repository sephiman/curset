# Exam style guide — Administrativo del Estado (C1), ingreso libre

Round R0a, 2026-10-16. Requirement R5.2: the style of authored questions is derived from the official exams, not assumed. This guide is the test a reviewer applies to every question in the bank.

**Sources.** Primary: the 2025 convocatoria. That is the ordinario of 23-05-2026 (modelo A; modelo B is the same questions in another order) and the extraordinario of 24-07-2026. Secondary: 2024 (ordinario modelo A and extraordinario), 2022 (ordinario and extraordinario) and OEP 2020 (ordinario and extraordinario; files `conv2020_*`, set under an earlier programme). Eight distinct questionnaires, 1,000 questions. Files: `content/age-administrativo-c1/sources/examenes-oficiales/`.

**Method.** Stem form, negative stems, «ninguna/todas» options, lengths and key position are counted mechanically from the questionnaire text, with the borderline matches checked by hand. Option type, distractor construction and tema were classified per question by reading it. All numbers are in the tables at the end. Where 2025 differs from earlier years, 2025 rules.

Quotations are exact, including the official typography.

## 1. Format

- Every question has **four options, a) to d), and exactly one correct answer** (all 1,000 questions; Anexo III 2.2: «sólo una de ellas es correcta»).
- Part 1: 70 questions plus 5 reserve. Positions 1–40 are bloques I–V and 41–70 are bloque VI, in every exam.
- In 2020, 2024 and 2025, positions 1–40 follow the programme order (bloque I, then II, III, IV, V). Only one or two questions fall out of place per exam. The 2022 exams mix the bloques.
- Reserve questions follow the same mix. Each exam has 0 to 2 bloque VI questions among the five.
- Part 2: two supuestos, each 20 questions plus 5 reserve. They cover bloques II–V, plus one bloque I question each in 2022 extr., 2024 ord. and 2024 extr. The candidate answers one supuesto.
- Questions are independent of each other in part 1. In part 2 most depend on the scenario, and a few depend on the previous question (section 11).

## 2. Stem forms

Four forms carry most of the exam. Shares are for the 2025 part 1 (ordinario + extraordinario, 150 questions); the tables give each exam.

| Form | 2025 part 1 | 2020–2024 part 1 | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| «De acuerdo con / Según / Conforme al artículo X de [norma], …:» (the stem is a sentence to complete) | 46 (31%) | 9–17% | «De acuerdo con el artículo 167 de la Constitución Española de 1978, los proyectos de reforma constitucional deberán ser aprobados:» |
| Direct question «¿…?» with no norm named | 46 (31%) | 11–33% | «¿Cuántos objetivos de desarrollo sostenible contiene la Agenda 2030?» |
| «¿Cuál de las siguientes…?» | 24 (16%) | 8–36% | — |
| «Según el artículo X …, ¿…?» (article named, then a question) | 16 (11%) | 0–11% | — |
| Statement to complete, no norm named | 9 (6%) | 7–16% | — |
| «Señale la respuesta correcta…» (or «FALSA», «INCORRECTA») | 4 (3%) | 0–9% | — |
| «Según la [norma]» without an article | 5 (3%) | 9–19% to complete, 3–8% as a question | — |

- **The article is named in the stem in 79 of the 150 part-1 questions of 2025 (53%).** In 2020–2024 the share is 15–33%. The trend is toward naming the article.
- The norm is cited with its full title the first time, as in the BOE: «Ley 39/2015, de 1 de octubre, del Procedimiento Administrativo Común de las Administraciones Públicas». Short forms appear when the exam has defined them («en adelante CE», «TREBEP», «LCSP»).
- **No option is ever an article number.** No question in 1,000 asks «¿qué artículo regula…?». The article is given in the stem and the options test its content.
- Ofimática stems name the application and version as the programme does: «En Outlook 365…», «En una hoja de Excel 365 en idioma español…», «En el Explorador de archivos de Windows 11…». Before 2024 they named Windows 10 and Office 2019.

- Plazos, procedures and dates matter more in part 2 than in part 1. Part 2 asks the candidate to apply the norm to the facts.
- Options in one question share a grammatical form and usually a length. They start with the same word when the stem invites it («Al…», «Por…», «Hasta el…»).

## 4. «Ninguna de las anteriores» / «Todas son correctas»

- **Not used in 2024 or 2025** (0 in 500 questions).
- In 2022 and 2020 there are 4 «Ninguna de las anteriores» and 1 «Todas son correctas» options in 1,000 questions. **None of them is the key.** One is in a question the tribunal annulled (2022 ordinario, supuesto II question 1).
- Combined options («a) y b) son correctas») never appear.

## 5. Negative stems

- **2025: none in part 1, one in part 2** (2025 ord., supuesto II, question 14: «Posteriormente al proceso selectivo de personal laboral el INAP quiere extinguir el convenio, ¿cuál de las siguientes respuestas NO se contempla en la normativa como causa de extinción de los convenios?»).
- 2024: 3 in the extraordinario, none in the ordinario. 2022 and 2020: 6–11 per exam in part 1.
- When used, the negation is in capitals: «NO», «FALSA», «INCORRECTA». Example (2024 extr., part 1, question 30):

  > Según establece el artículo 95 del texto refundido de la Ley del Estatuto Básico del Empleado Público, ¿cuál de las siguientes opciones NO es una falta muy grave?
  > a) La realización de actos encaminados a coartar el libre ejercicio del derecho de huelga.
  > b) La intervención en un procedimiento administrativo cuando se dé alguna de las causas de abstención legalmente señaladas.
  > c) La incomparecencia injustificada en las Comisiones de Investigación de las Cortes Generales y de las asambleas legislativas de las comunidades autónomas.
  > d) El abandono del servicio, así como no hacerse cargo voluntariamente de las tareas o funciones que tienen encomendadas.

  Key: b.

- The trend is away from negative stems. Keep them rare: at most 1 in 20 authored questions, always in capitals.

## 6. How distractors are built

Shares are of 2025 part-1 questions, by the dominant pattern of the three wrong options.

| Pattern | 2025 part 1 | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Texto alterado**: the norm's own sentence with a word added, removed or swapped | 39 (26%) | 2025 ord. part 1, question 3 (below) |
| **Otra figura del mismo tipo**: a sibling concept (another recurso, situación, clase de contrato, órgano colegiado) | 44 (29%) | «La revocación de la autorización» / «La declaración de lesividad» / «La rectificación de errores» |
| **Invención**: plausible-sounding text that is in no norm, or a wrong ofimática behaviour | 29 (19%) | Mostly ofimática and general-knowledge questions |
| **Otro plazo / cifra cercana**: another plazo from the same norm, or a nearby number | 15 (10%) | 2025 ord. part 1, question 19 (section 3) |
| **Órgano de otro nivel**: the organ one level up or down, or a parallel organ | 5 (3%) | 2025 ord. part 1, question 8: «a) Los Secretarios de Estado. b) Los Subsecretarios. c) Los Directores Generales. d) Los Subdirectores Generales.» |
| **Otra aplicación / comando**: a real command or feature that does something else | 8 (5%) | Ofimática |
| **Norma anterior**: the wording before an amendment | 0 in 2025; 2 in 1,000 | 2020 ord., supuesto II question 19: «b) Seis meses dentro de un periodo de doce meses.» against the key «c) Nueve meses dentro de un periodo de dieciocho meses.» (TREBEP art. 10 after Ley 20/2021) |
| Mixed | 10 (7%) | — |

**«Texto alterado» example** (2025 ord., part 1, question 3; key a):

> De acuerdo con el artículo 67 de la Constitución Española de 1978:
> a) Nadie podrá ser miembro de las dos Cámaras simultáneamente.
> b) Nadie podrá ser miembro de las dos Cámaras simultáneamente, salvo el Presidente del Gobierno y los Ministros.
> c) Nadie podrá ser miembro de las dos Cámaras simultáneamente, salvo el Presidente del Gobierno.
> d) Nadie podrá ser miembro de las dos Cámaras simultáneamente, pero se podrá acumular el acta de una Asamblea de Comunidad Autónoma con la de Diputado al Congreso.

Rules the official exams follow and the bank must follow:

- The correct option is usually the norm's wording verbatim, or very close to it. The distractors are built from that same wording.
- Distractors in one question come from one pattern, or two at most. Four plazos, four organs, four variants of one sentence.
- Numbers in a quantity question are near each other and are real figures from neighbouring provisions where possible: 300/350/250, three fifths / absolute majority.
- The previous version of an amended rule is a legitimate distractor (R5.3), but the official exams almost never use it. Use it sparingly, and only where the amendment is itself examinable.

## 7. Lengths

| | 2025 part 1 | 2020–2024 part 1 | 2025 part 2 | 2020–2024 part 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stem words, mean | 29–30 | 23–29 | 35–37 | 29–40 |
| Option words, mean | 13 | 10–14 | 10–17 | 12–17 |

- **The key is the longest option in 45–52% of the 2025 part-1 questions**, against 25% by chance. Across all exams and both parts the share is 32–58%. The official exams have this bias; the bank must not copy it (checklist item 9).
- The official keys are spread across a–d without a pattern (tables). The platform shuffles options in the bank (R5.6).

## 8. Questions per tema

The full table is at the end. Summary for 2025 (ordinario + extraordinario, both parts, 250 questions):

- Bloque III (derecho administrativo) and bloque VI carry the most: 62 questions each. 47 of bloque III's are in part 2.
- Bloque IV (personal): 47. Bloque V (gestión financiera): 37. Bloque I: 28, all in part 1. Bloque II: 14. Bloque VI: 62, all in part 1.
- In part 1 each tema of bloque I gets 1–2 questions per exam, occasionally 0 or 3–4 (2022 extr.). In 2024–2025 no tema of bloque I is skipped.
- In part 2, III.3 (procedimiento, recursos) and III.4 (contratos) appear in every exam, with 3–12 questions each. IV.4–IV.6 (situaciones; provisión, derechos y permisos; régimen disciplinario) and V.2–V.3 (modificaciones presupuestarias; ejecución del gasto) appear in almost every exam.

The 2020 exams were set under an earlier programme. Their columns are «programa anterior» and are not weights for the 2025 programme.

## 9. Ofimática (bloque VI)

The full index is `docs/age-ofimatica-index.md`. Style points:

- The stem names the application and its version («Outlook 365», «Excel 365», «Windows 11»). Options use the Spanish-locale labels exactly: tab names, group names, commands, function names (BUSCARV, CONTARA, SIERROR).
- Forms: concept («¿Qué es…?»), behaviour («¿qué ocurre si…?»), menu path («Pestaña Inicio > grupo Edición > …»), shortcut, formula result. Formula-result questions give the formula in the stem and four results as options, including error values («#¿NOMBRE?», «#N/A»).
- Example (2025 ord., part 1, question 65; key d):

  > En Outlook 365, en la barra de búsqueda escribimos el texto: cc:"Ana Muñoz", ¿cuál de las siguientes opciones indica de forma correcta qué mensajes devolverá la búsqueda de Outlook?
  > a) Devolverá los elementos en los que la dirección de correo electrónico tenga como nombre anamunoz, (por ejemplo, anamunoz@outlook.com), en la línea cc (con copia).
  > b) No devolverá ningún elemento ya que buscará aquellos que en la línea cc (con copia), tengan en la dirección de correo el texto Ana Muñoz, lo que no es correcto en una dirección de correo.
  > c) Devolverá los elementos en los que Ana Muñoz está en la línea cc (con copia), solo si tenemos este nombre, con el correo electrónico, entre nuestros contactos de Outlook.
  > d) Devolverá los elementos en los que el nombre Ana Muñoz esté en la línea cc (con copia).

- The official questions sometimes rely on features of Word para la web, of the new Outlook or of Windows 10. The bank uses Windows 11 and Microsoft 365 desktop only (Anexo III 2.1). It does not ask about a feature that differs between classic and new Outlook without saying which one.

## 11. Part 2: the supuestos

- **Length.** One scenario of 225–791 words. The 2025 scenarios run 272–532 words; read 2025 as the target, about 300–550 words.
- **Shape.** A narrative in the past or present tense. A named fictional person («Doña Clara Hevia», «Don Eduardo Merino») deals with a real AGE body: a confederación hidrográfica, the INAP, a ministry, the AEMET, the INE. The scenario packs several unconnected threads into one story: a citizen's request, a file, a contract, a budget shortage, a staff situation. Each thread feeds a run of questions in bloques III, IV and V.
- **How facts are stated.** Facts are given as plain statements with exact figures: dates with the weekday («el lunes, 3 de marzo de 2025»), amounts in euros, budget items («subconcepto 226.07», «aplicación presupuestaria 33.101.921O.227.06»), the person's corps and post. The scenario ends with «Se plantean las siguientes cuestiones:».
- **Questions add facts.** Many stems bring in a new fact the scenario does not contain («Doña Clara Hevia recibe la notificación de subsanación de su solicitud de autorización el martes, 3 de junio de 2025.»). So a question is answerable from scenario + stem + the norm.
- **Dependence.** Most questions depend on the scenario only for context: the norm answers them. The per-question review flagged 1–4 per exam that depend on the previous question or need a computation. One example is «¿Cómo se financiará dicho suplemento?», which follows the question that establishes the suplemento de crédito. Stems with a date to compute from are frequent in 2025: 9 and 18 of the 50 part-2 questions. Example (2025 ord., supuesto I, question 20; key c):

  > ¿Hasta qué fecha tendrá la Secretaria General para tomar posesión en su nuevo puesto si se tiene en cuenta que el cese en su puesto actual se produce el lunes, 3 de marzo de 2025, y que le supondrá un cambio de residencia de Valladolid a Madrid?
  > a) Hasta el martes, 4 de marzo de 2025.
  > b) Hasta el miércoles, 5 de marzo de 2025.
  > c) Hasta el jueves, 3 de abril de 2025.
  > d) Hasta el viernes, 4 de abril de 2025.

- **Spread.** Per 50 part-2 questions (two supuestos plus reserves): bloque III 16–27, IV 12–18, V 10–16, II 0–5.
- **Faults to avoid.** The official cases contain slips the bank must not copy: a person renamed mid-case (2022 extr.: «Lucas Nuño» / «Luis Núñez»), the wrong title for a law (2025 ord. supuesto I, question 2 calls Ley 39/2015 «de Régimen Jurídico del Sector Público»), ministry names that changed before the exam date.

## 12. Reviewer checklist

Apply to every authored question. A question that fails any item is dropped (R5.4), not fixed in place.

1. Four options, a)–d), exactly one correct by the cited text. No other option is defensible under the same text or under another norm in force.
2. The stem names the norm with its BOE title and, where the question rests on one article, the article («De acuerdo con el artículo 87 de la Ley 39/2015, de 1 de octubre, del Procedimiento Administrativo Común de las Administraciones Públicas, …»). Ofimática stems name the application and version (Windows 11, Word/Excel/Access/Outlook 365).
3. The stem is a sentence to complete ending in «:» or a question «¿…?». It uses one of the forms of section 2.
4. No option is an article number. No option is «Ninguna de las anteriores», «Todas son correctas» or a combination of letters.
5. A negative stem («NO», «FALSA», «INCORRECTA») is in capitals, and there is no more than one per 20 questions of a tema.
6. The correct option is the norm's wording or a faithful reduction of it. Every number, plazo, majority and organ matches the consolidated text named in `sources/normativa.yaml`, in the version in force on the exam date.
7. Distractors follow one or two patterns of section 6: the norm's sentence altered, a sibling figure, another plazo or nearby number from the same norm, the organ one level off, the previous wording of an amended rule. Each distractor is wrong by the cited text, not merely unusual.
8. The four options share grammatical form and register. None is absurd, a joke, or wrong on spelling alone.
9. The key is not systematically the longest option. Across a tema's questions it is the longest in no more than about a quarter.
10. Stem 15–40 words in part 1 and 20–50 words in a case. Options 5–20 words. A statement-type option may run to 40 words when the norm's sentence does.
11. Organ and ministry names are the current ones (RD 829/2023 and later), unless the question is about a dated fact and says so.
12. Ofimática: the feature exists in Windows 11 / Microsoft 365 desktop as of the round's date, and labels are the Spanish-locale labels. Nothing that exists only in Windows 10, Word para la web or one of the two Outlooks, unless the stem says which.
13. Case questions: the scenario plus the stem state every fact needed. Persons are fictional; bodies are real AGE bodies with their current names. A question that needs the previous one says so («dicho suplemento») and the pair is kept together.
14. Dates in a case give the weekday and the year, and computations are checked against the calendar of that year (holidays stated in the stem if relevant).
15. The question is not an official question copied or lightly reworded (R5.5). Compare with `sources/examenes-oficiales/`.
16. Spanish is Peninsular and correct. There are no typos, missing accents or stray characters; the official exams have some, and the bank does not copy them.
17. Citation, explanation, difficulty and tags are present (R5.3). The explanation says why the key is right and why each distractor is wrong.
