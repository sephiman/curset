Observations that do not change a verdict:

- q013–q025 name RD 366/2007 as «Real Decreto 366/2007, de 16 de marzo», without the descriptive title (q013 has
  to drop it, because the title is the answer). q010–q012 name the RDL 1/2013 by the Texto Refundido's title without
  «Real Decreto Legislativo 1/2013». Both forms appear in the official exams (conv2022 P1 3, conv2020_X P1 20; the
  TREBEP example in style § 5), so I did not treat them as a checklist-2 failure.
- q016 and q021: the explanations refer to the article's letters («La letra a) garantiza…», «La letra b) pide…»).
  They name the options by content, so the letter rule is met, but after shuffling a reader may take «la letra b)»
  for an option. A coordinator applying the letter rule more strictly would drop both.
- q012 option «En los plazos que fije cada departamento ministerial mediante orden»: an orden ministerial is a
  reglamento, but «cada departamento» does not match «establecidos reglamentariamente» as the text frames it. I kept it
  as wrong by the text.

Lesson sentences at odds with the norm: none found in the sections this batch covers. One minor point: the lesson
quotes art. 9.1 RD 208/1996 with «de los Gobiernos Civiles» and no «hoy» note (now Subdelegaciones del Gobierno),
unlike its other old organ names. That is accurate to the norm, but inconsistent with the lesson's own convention.
