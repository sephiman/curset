# Mix sheet — m02-l01: norm-completion variants (Juanjo decision 5, 2026-11-07)

Kept 76. Write **about 8** stems of the form «según la norma (sin artículo) … (completar)»: the norm named by its BOE title, **no article number in the stem**, a sentence to complete ending in «:» (2025's dominant II.1–II.4 form, 58 %). Each tests a fact the bank already asks in article form, listed below, from a **new angle or the same angle in this form**, with new distractors (never a copy of the listed question's options).

(art. 4.1 RDL 1/2013)

(art. 8.2.c) RD 366/2007)

(art. 4.e) RD 208/1996)
