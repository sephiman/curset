## Choices that the verifier should know

- No recurso question against a MUFACE resolution. RD 577/1997 disposición adicional primera says that resolutions on mutualismo by the competent organ do not end the vía administrativa (recurso ordinario before the Minister), while art. 6.3 says the Presidencia's acts do; the lesson cites neither, so the case avoids alzada/reposición on MUFACE acts.
- Budget thread uses LGP and RD 2188/1995 (both reach an organismo autónomo), never the IOC. RDL 4/2000 art. 5.2 sends MUFACE's economic regime to its own law and the LGP «en las materias que sea de aplicación»; the questions are on generación, incorporación, fiscalización of a supply contract and anticipos de caja fija, which are general OA rules, not the control of prestaciones.
- Mutualistas are staff of Ministerios (Hacienda, Trabajo y Economía Social); MUFACE's own staff (Don Nicolás, Doña Irene, Doña Carmen, Don Rubén) carry no mutualista statement, because RDL 4/2000 art. 3.2.b excludes «los funcionarios de organismos autónomos».
- q11 counts the month from the fecha de efectos (art. 10.3 RD 375/2003) with art. 30.4–5 LPAC, as the lesson says; the last day is the festivo of 1 November and passes to Tuesday 2 November. RD 375/2003 art. 10.5 (no option when the excedencia comes from passing to another cuerpo of the Administración Civil) does not apply: the excedencia is por interés particular.
- q08: «dos meses antes» of 30 September 2029 is 30 July 2029; the contract ends on a Sunday and the preaviso date is a Monday. The month count is backwards from the end date (the lesson does not say how); the end date has the same day number in both months, so no end-of-month ambiguity.
- q07 uses art. 50.1 (the plazo from the day after the notification); DA 15.ª LCSP (plazo from the sending of the notification) is not asked; the scenario says «se notifica».
- q18, q19: the stems say «contando días hábiles» because RD 2188/1995 arts. 13.2 and 16.1 do not say which days; q19 crosses 1 November (national) and 9 November (Madrid, stated in the scenario).
- q06 uses 140.000 euros (art. 22.1.a LCSP, Orden HAC/1517/2025, in force 2026–2027); the official conv2024_A·S2·14 asks the same article with a figure that the order changed; the key here is the reason (the valor estimado is not below the limit for organismos autónomos).
