## Lesson notes (no verdict changed)

- Lines 260–261 («Las excedencias voluntarias … su tiempo no computa a efectos de promoción, trienios y derechos
  pasivos (art. 19 RD 365/1995)») leave out art. 29.3 Ley 30/1984, kept in force by the BOE note: it counts
  for trienios the services in organismos or entidades del sector público, except those in sociedades mercantiles
  with a public majority holding. This is a «both rules» difference of the kind the theory ledger records for IV.4.
- Lines 254–255 give only the RD rule for the incentivada (interés particular if reingreso is not asked «dentro del
  mes siguiente», art. 18.4 RD 365/1995). Art. 29.7 Ley 30/1984 says «se pasará automáticamente, si no se solicita el
  reingreso», with no one-month window. This is not a conflict on what was asked, but it is a second wording of the
  same rule.
