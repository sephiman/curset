## Lesson/norm disagreements

No fact in the lesson contradicts the norm. Two citation problems the platform may reject:

1. **Structure (q002, q003).** The «Estructura» paragraph has no citation, and the «Lo que más cae» line 6 ends in
   «(CE)», which has no lead (`art.`, `disposición`…), so the citation grammar does not read it as a citation. Both
   questions carry the citation `CE`. If the platform rejects them, the lesson needs a citable parenthetical there.
2. **Reforms (q009, q010).** The lesson cites «(sources/otros/boe-analisis-ce.xml)», not
   «(fuente: `boe-analisis-ce.xml`)». The grammar only reads the `fuente:` form, so the lesson may not count as citing
   the file. The questions carry `fuente: \`boe-analisis-ce.xml\``. The fix would be in the lesson's parenthetical.

Also found, outside the lesson: in `official/I.1.md` the four 2020 entries (conv2020 · 1 and 2, conv2020_X · 1 and
3) show the questionnaire's cover instructions instead of the questions. I read the questions from the cuestionario
PDFs: conv2020 · 1 asks whether the CE has been reformed, conv2020 · 2 asks which right can be suspended (huelga),
conv2020_X · 1 asks about art. 13.2, conv2020_X · 3 asks where justice emanates from (art. 117.1).

The BOE text of art. 2.1 LO 3/1981 writes «del senado» in lowercase. The explanation of q048 does not quote it.
