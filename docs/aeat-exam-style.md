# Exam style guide — Agentes de la Hacienda Pública (AEAT), acceso libre, first exercise

Round R0a, 2026-11-11. Requirements R4.3 and R6.1: the depth of the theory (stage 1) and the style of authored questions
(stage 2) come from the official first-exercise questionnaires, not from assumptions. In stage 1 this guide tells the
theory writers what gets asked and how (sections 2–9); in stage 2, section 12 is the test a reviewer applies to every
question in the bank.

**Sources.** Primary: OEP 2025 (21-03-2026, tipo A; tipo B is the same 80 questions in another order). Secondary:
OEP 2024 (05-04-2025), OEP 2023 (20-04-2024), OEP 2022 (22-04-2023) and its extraordinario (18-07-2023), OEP 2021
(28-05-2022) and OEP 2020 (25-09-2021). Seven distinct questionnaires, 680 questions. Files:
`content/aeat-agentes-hacienda/sources/examenes-oficiales/`. Every question, its tema and status:
`docs/aeat-official-exams-ledger.md`.

**Method.** Lengths, key position, «ninguna/todas» options and the order of the parts are counted from the text
(`docs/age-verification/aeat/r0a/checks/textstats.json`). Stem form, option type, distractor construction, negative stem
and tema were classified per question by two readers, each checking the key against the consolidated norm
(`classification/`, `checks/style_stats.json`, `checks/style_by_part.txt`). Where 2025 differs from earlier years, 2025
rules: it is the first exam of the 32-tema programme and the 80-question format.

**Parts.** The programme has three parts (course codes): I Organización del Estado y funcionamiento de la AGE (I.1–I.7),
II Derecho Administrativo General (II.1–II.5), III Organización de la Hacienda Pública y Derecho Tributario
(III.1–III.20). Sections 2–7 give the figures per part where they differ.

Quotations are exact, including the official typography.

## 1. Format

- **Four options, A) to D), one correct** in all 680 questions (BOE-A-2025-27056, base 6.2: «preguntas con cuatro
  respuestas alternativas, de las cuales sólo una de ellas es la correcta»). A wrong answer costs a quarter of a right
  one; a blank costs nothing.
- **2025: 80 questions in 90 minutes, no reserve questions.** 2020–2024: 100 questions (60 minutes in OEP 2020–2021,
  1 h 40 in OEP 2022–2024). No exam has reserve questions.
- **The parts are mixed, not in blocks**, in 2023, 2024 and 2025: a part III question can be question 1 and a part I
  question can be question 79. Only OEP 2022 (ordinario) runs the programme in order (I, then II, then III).
- **Share of each part.** 2025: I 8, II 10, III 62 (78 % tax). OEP 2022–2024: I 15–18, II 11–15, III 63–67 per 100,
  plus 2–8 questions on topics the 2025 programme dropped. The 2025 exam moved weight to the tax part.
- Option letters are capitals with «)» in 2025 («A)»); earlier exams print «A-», «A.-» or «A.». Numbering «01.-» (2025),
  «1.-» (2023–2024), «1.» (2020–2022).
- Questions are independent. There are no grouped cases in the first exercise; a «supuesto breve» is a one-question
  scenario (section 9).

## 2. Stem forms

| Form | 2025 | OEP 2022–2024 (per 100) | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| «De acuerdo con / Según / Conforme al artículo X de [norma], …:» (sentence to complete) | 31 (39 %) | 30–50 | 2025, 2: «De acuerdo con el artículo 29 del Real Decreto 1065/2007, de 27 de julio, […] deberán asentarse en los correspondientes registros en el plazo de:» |
| «Señale la respuesta / afirmación CORRECTA / INCORRECTA», with the article named | 18 (23 %) | 16–28 | 2025, 4: «De acuerdo con el artículo 56 de la Ley 58/2003, de 17 de diciembre, General Tributaria, señale la respuesta CORRECTA sobre la cuota tributaria:» |
| Direct question «¿…?» | 9 (11 %) | 2–7 | 2025, 19: «¿Cuál es el plazo que establece la Ley Orgánica 2/1979 […] para interponer un recurso de amparo constitucional […]?» |
| Supuesto breve with data (section 9) | 8 (10 %) | 5–13 | 2025, 5 (section 9) |
| «Según el artículo X …, ¿…?» | 7 (9 %) | 13–22 | — |
| «¿Cuál de las siguientes…?» | 7 (9 %) | 2–12 | — |
| Norm named without an article; statement to complete with no norm | 0 | 1–5 | — |

- **The article is named in the stem in 73 of the 80 questions of 2025 (91 %).** OEP 2022–2024: 73–97 per 100.
  OEP 2020–2021: 54 and 73. Part III names it most (281 of 324 in OEP 2022–2025), then I (63 of 74) and II (59 of 64).
- The norm is cited with its full BOE title, usually including the date: «Ley 58/2003, de 17 de diciembre, General
  Tributaria»; «Real Decreto 939/2005, de 29 de julio, por el que se aprueba el Reglamento General de Recaudación». Short
  names («LGT», «RGR») do not appear in 2025 stems.
- **The CORRECTA/INCORRECTA instruction is in capitals** and often comes after the stem has set the topic: «El
  artículo 73 del Real Decreto 1065/2007 […] regula la expedición de los certificados tributarios. En relación con
  dichos certificados, indique cuál de las siguientes afirmaciones es CORRECTA:» (2025, 62).
- No option is an article number, and no question asks «¿qué artículo regula…?».

## 3. What the options are

| Option type | 2025 | OEP 2022–2025, part I / II / III |
|---|---|---|
| Full statements (the norm's wording, one detail changed) | 55 (69 %) | 61 % / 75 % / 64 % |
| Plazos | 6 | 8 % / 16 % / 12 % |
| Organs | 4 | **18 %** / 0 % / 3 % |
| Dates (devengo, start of a plazo, end of a period) | 2 | 1 % / 0 % / 4 % |
| Percentages and amounts | 2 | 1 % / 0 % / 5 % |
| Calculations (a quota, a reduction, a recargo) | 3 | 0 % / 0 % / 2 % |
| Subjects (who is sujeto pasivo, responsable, legitimado) | 2 | 3 % / 5 % / 3 % |
| Procedures and other | 6 | — |

- Statements dominate. A statement option is often long: the norm's sentence copied with one element changed
  (section 6). Medians: stem 32–38 words, option 13–21 words (section 7).
- Options in one question share a grammatical form, and very often the same opening words. 2025, 19: «El de los quince
  días siguientes… / El de los veinte días siguientes… / El del mes siguiente… / El de los tres meses siguientes a la
  notificación de la resolución recaída en el previo proceso judicial.»
- Part I asks for organs more than the other parts: who appoints, who controls, who is competent. 2025, 46: «A la
  Intervención General de la Administración del Estado. / Al Tribunal de Cuentas. / Al Tribunal
  Económico-Administrativo Regional. / Al Tribunal Superior de Justicia de la Comunidad Autónoma en la que se
  encuadre.» (key B).

## 4. «Ninguna de las anteriores» / «Todas son correctas»

- **None in 680 questions**, and no combined option («A y B son correctas»). Do not use either form.

## 5. Negative stems

- **2025: 2 of 80**, one in capitals and one not:
  - 2025, 65: «El artículo 99 de la Ley 35/2006 […] regula la obligación de practicar pagos a cuenta del impuesto.
    Indique sobre que rentas NO existirá obligación de retener […]» (key D).
  - 2025, 12: «Conforme al artículo 158 del Reglamento (UE) nº 952/2013 […], ¿para cuál de los siguientes regímenes no es
    necesario la presentación de una declaración en aduana?» (key C; lower-case «no»).
- OEP 2022–2024: 4–15 per 100, most in part II (14 of 64) and III (30 of 324); part I has 3 of 74. OEP 2020–2021: 8–16.
- The negation is usually in capitals: «NO», «INCORRECTA», «FALSA». The trend is down. Keep them at most 1 in 20 and
  always in capitals.

## 6. How distractors are built

| Pattern | 2025 | OEP 2022–2025, part I / II / III |
|---|---|---|
| **Texto alterado**: the norm's sentence with a word, a condition or a number changed | 28 (35 %) | 36 % / 52 % / 31 % |
| **Otra figura del mismo tipo**: a sibling concept (another recargo, another procedure, another tax, another régimen aduanero) | 26 (33 %) | 23 % / 25 % / 33 % |
| **Otro plazo**: another plazo from the same norm or a neighbouring one | 7 | 8 % / 17 % / 11 % |
| **Cifra cercana**: a nearby percentage or amount | 2 | 4 % / 0 % / 6 % |
| **Órgano de otro nivel** | 3 | 12 % / 0 % / 3 % |
| **Cálculo erróneo**: the right method with one step wrong | 1 | 0 / 0 / 2 % |
| **Invención** | 5 | 4 % / 5 % / 2 % |
| Mixed | 8 | 12 % / 2 % / 12 % |

**«Texto alterado», plazos** (2025, 2; key A): the four options are the same RGAT sentence with «Un mes», «Seis
meses», «Dos meses» and «Tres meses».

**«Texto alterado», condition swapped** (2025, 3; key D): «Las deudas de vencimiento posterior a la declaración de
fallido se considerarán vencidas y podrán ser dadas de baja por referencia a dicha declaración, si no existen otros
obligados al pago.» Distractors change «posterior» to «anterior», «si no existen» to «aunque existan», or the ending
to «si el obligado al pago lo solicita expresamente».

**«Otra figura del mismo tipo», concept against concept** (2025, 4; key A): the correct definition of cuota íntegra
against the definitions of the cuota líquida and of other parts of art. 56 LGT, attributed to the cuota íntegra.

Rules the official exams follow and the bank must follow:

- The key is the norm's wording verbatim, or very close to it. The distractors are built from the same wording.
- One pattern per question, two at most: four plazos, four percentages, four variants of one sentence.
- Numbers are real figures from neighbouring provisions: the recargos of art. 27 LGT (1 % per month, 5 %, 10 %, 15 %,
  20 %), the plazos of art. 62 LGT, the IRPF reductions of art. 23.2.
- The previous version of an amended rule is a legitimate distractor only where the amendment is itself examinable
  (R5.3 of C1). The official exams do not use it on purpose; old questions become wrong when the law changes (ledger,
  `norma modificada`).

## 7. Lengths and keys

| | 2025 | OEP 2022–2024 | OEP 2020–2021 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stem words, median | 32.5 | 35–38 | 29.5–30.5 |
| Stem words, max | 108 | 86–121 | 82–115 |
| Option words, median | 18 | 15–21 | 13–16 |
| Option words, max | 80 | 61–111 | 68–76 |

- Part III stems are the longest (median 38 words) because they name the article and its regulation; options median
  19 words.
- **The key is the longest option in 37 of the 80 questions of 2025 (46 %)**, against 25 % by chance. Earlier exams:
  34–46 %. The literal text of the norm tends to be longer than the altered versions. The bank must not copy this bias
  (checklist item 9).
- Keys spread across A–D: 2025 A 18, B 19, C 20, D 23. Earlier exams are less even (2021: A 15, B 35). The platform
  shuffles options.

The full table is in the ledger. Summary for the last four convocatorias (OEP 2022–2025 with the 2022 extraordinario,
480 questions; 18 more are `fuera de temario`):

- **Part III carries the exam.** Most asked: III.4 (obligados tributarios, obligaciones formales, domicilio,
  prescripción) 32; III.12 (embargo, responsables, sucesores) 24; III.15 (IRPF I) 24; III.8 (inspección) 23; III.5
  (información, colaboración, normas comunes, notificaciones) 22; III.13 (sanciones) 20; III.19 (IVA II) 19; III.14
  (revisión) 18.
- Least asked in part III: III.6 (declaraciones, autoliquidaciones, pagos a cuenta) 8 and III.17 (Impuesto sobre
  Sociedades) 8. Both are 2025 temas whose content earlier programmes spread differently (Sociedades had no tema of its
  own in OEP 2023); 2025 asked 3 and 4.
- **Every tema of part III appeared in 2025 except III.12** (0 in 2025, 4–11 in each earlier exam).
- Parts I and II: 1–4 questions per tema per exam. In 2025, I.5 and I.6 had none; each other common tema had 1–3.
- 2025 by tema: III.13, III.18 and III.19 5 each; III.4, III.5, III.14, III.15, III.17 and III.20 4 each.

OEP 2020 and 2021 are in the ledger but outside these totals (requirement R4.6 asks for the last four convocatorias).

## 9. Tax questions: figures, plazos, dates and short cases

The tax part replaces C1's ofimática section. What it asks:

- **Plazos and dates are the core of III.4–III.14.** Prescripción (4 years and its computation), plazos de pago (art.
  62 LGT), plazos de resolución (6 months by default, art. 104.1 LGT), duración de las actuaciones inspectoras (art.
  150 LGT), plazos of recursos and reclamaciones (one month), plazos of the RGAT (registro de operaciones, certificados:
  20 days).
- **Percentages:** recargos por declaración extemporánea (art. 27 LGT), recargos del período ejecutivo (5/10/20 %, art.
  28), reducciones de sanciones (art. 188), IRPF reductions on rent (art. 23.2), IVA prorrata.
- **Supuesto breve with data:** a one-paragraph scenario with a fictional company or person, dates and amounts, then a
  question. 8 in 2025, 5–13 per earlier exam. Example (2025, 5; key C):

  > Una sociedad española vende, a través de un contrato de venta con pacto de reserva de dominio suscrito el 1 de mayo,
  > una maquinaria industrial a otra empresa española. El 1 de junio se entrega la maquinaria en las instalaciones del
  > comprador, y se pacta un pago parcial del 50% el 1 de julio y el resto el 1 de agosto. ¿Cuándo se produce el/los
  > devengo/s de la operación, según el artículo 75 de la Ley 37/1992, del Impuesto sobre el Valor Añadido?
  > A) La operación se devenga por el 50% del importe con el pago parcial el 1 de julio, y por el otro 50% con el pago
  > final el 1 de agosto.
  > B) La operación se devenga el 1 de agosto con el pago final, por ser el momento en que transmite la propiedad debido
  > a la condición suspensiva del pacto de reserva de dominio.
  > C) La operación se devenga el 1 de junio, cuando la maquinaria se pone en posesión del adquirente.
  > D) La operación se devenga el 1 de mayo con la suscripción del contrato.

- **Calculations** are rare (3 in 2025) and short: one rule applied once (a quota, a recargo), with round numbers.
- **Figures set by yearly norms** (interés de demora, IPREM, módulos) were the answer of 2 questions in 680 (2020-68
  and 2022-87, IRPF filing thresholds of art. 96 LIRPF). The exams ask the figures fixed in the tax laws themselves,
  which change by reform rather than every year. Lessons still state every annual figure with its year and norm (R3.5);
  `sources/normativa.yaml`, field `cifras_anuales`.
- **The AEAT's organisation (III.2)** is asked through the Orden de 2 de junio de 1994, Orden PRE/3581/2007 and the
  Resoluciones de 13 de enero de 2021: which departamento or dependencia does what, the rank of the Director General.
- **Customs (III.20)** questions cite the Código Aduanero de la Unión (Reglamento (UE) 952/2013) by article:
  representación aduanera, regímenes, declaración, deuda aduanera.

## 12. Reviewer checklist (stage 2)

Apply to every authored question. A question that fails any item is dropped, not fixed in place. In stage 1 the same
items tell the theory writer what the exam needs from each lesson: the exact plazos, percentages, organs and
conditions of the cited articles, with their article numbers.

1. Four options, A)–D), exactly one correct by the cited text. No other option is defensible under the same text or
   another norm in force.
2. The stem names the norm with its BOE title and date and, where the question rests on one article, the article.
3. The stem is a sentence to complete ending in «:», a question «¿…?», or a «señale la respuesta CORRECTA» instruction;
   a supuesto breve states every fact needed (dates, amounts, who is who).
4. No option is an article number, «Ninguna de las anteriores», «Todas son correctas» or a combination of letters.
5. A negative stem is in capitals («NO», «INCORRECTA», «FALSA»), at most one per 20 questions of a tema.
6. The key is the norm's wording or a faithful reduction. Every plazo, percentage, amount, organ and condition matches
   the consolidated text in `sources/normativa.yaml`, in the version in force on the exam date; a `cambio-pendiente`
   provision uses the later version and is tagged.
7. Distractors follow one or two patterns of section 6. Each is wrong by the cited text, not merely odd. Numbers are
   real figures from neighbouring provisions.
8. The four options share grammatical form and register; none is absurd or wrong only by spelling.
9. The key is not systematically the longest option: across a tema, no more than about a quarter.
10. Stem 20–60 words (a supuesto breve up to 110). Options 5–30 words; a statement option may run to 60 words when the
    norm's sentence does.
11. Organ names are the current ones (AEAT departments and dependencias per the orders in `normativa.yaml`).
12. A figure set by a yearly norm (interés de demora, interés legal, IPREM, módulos, aplazamiento guarantee limit) names
    its year in the stem or is avoided; the explanation cites the norm that sets it for that year.
13. A supuesto breve uses fictional taxpayers and states every date with the year; computations are checked against the
    norm step by step and against the calendar (art. 30 LPAC / art. 48 LGT counting).
14. The topic is in the 2025 programme. IRNR, Impuestos Especiales, contrabando, impuestos medioambientales, IVA tipos
    impositivos, retenciones, declaraciones informativas and the consulta tributaria are not (programa.md, «Cambios»),
    unless a 2025 tema phrase covers the point and the question says how.
15. Not an official question copied or lightly reworded (compare with `sources/examenes-oficiales/` and section 10).
16. Peninsular Spanish, no typos; the official errata («Ley 58/2023», «Ley 27/2004», «Ley 29/1988», «Ley Orgánica
    3/2017», «Ley 58/2003, de 28 de diciembre») are not copied.
17. Citation, explanation, difficulty and tags present; the explanation says why the key is right and why each distractor
    is wrong.
