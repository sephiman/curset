## 1. Scenario

The scenario runs to 549 words. All 11 dated phrases have the right weekday for 2027, and the commas after the year
are in place. The people and the companies are invented. The bodies have their current names: Subdelegación del
Gobierno en Valladolid (MPTMD), Delegación de Economía y Hacienda, Cuerpo de Gestión de la Administración Civil del
Estado and Cuerpo General Administrativo. The timeline holds and the figures fit: 48.000 is under the 60.000 of
art. 159.6, and 43.500 is under 48.000. The festivos sentence limits itself to «plazos por días» and to Easter in
month-counted plazos. Every window counted by days was checked against the 2027 national festivos and none falls
in one. One `SCENARIO` fault:

- **SCENARIO (incomplete).** «pierde el miércoles, 9 de junio de 2027, a su suegra, que vivía en Zamora». Art. 48.a
  TREBEP turns on whether «el suceso se produzca en la misma localidad». The scenario says where the suegra lived.
  It does not say where she died or where Doña Elena lives. This changes the key of q11: three days, ending on the
  Monday 14 option, if the death is in the same locality.

Note that changes no verdict: q17's `depends_on` quotes «la orden de comisión le autoriza el automóvil propio»,
which is not in the scenario. The scenario says «y automóvil propio autorizado».
