| overview | PASS | The only legal statement (título IV LGT, arts. 178 a 212, potestad sancionadora) holds: the extracted text shows art. 212 closing the title and título V starting after it. The rest describes structure. |
| after:### Principios aplicables#1 | PASS | Matches art. 178: administrative sanctioning principles with LGT specialities, the five named principles, and irretroactividad "con carácter general" subject to art. 10.2. |
| after:### Principio de responsabilidad#1 | FAIL | "si el autor carece de capacidad de obrar" drops the limit: art. 179.2.a says "capacidad de obrar en el orden tributario", which is narrower than general capacity to act, so the reader would exempt more people than the article does. Minor point: art. 179.3 also keeps any infracciones caused by late or incorrect filing of the new declarations, and the paragraph leaves that out. |
| after:### Principio de no concurrencia#1 | PASS | Matches art. 180.1 (a conduct that must be used for graduation or qualification is not sanctioned separately), 180.2 (several conducts lead to several sanctions) and 180.3 (sanctions are compatible with interés de demora and recargos del período ejecutivo). |
| after:### Responsables y sucesores de las sanciones#1 | PASS | Solidaria liability (art. 42.1.a and c, and 42.2), subsidiaria liability (art. 43.1.a, g and h), no transfer to heirs and legatees, and transfer to the successors of dissolved companies all match art. 182. |
| after:### Infracciones tributarias#1 | PASS | Matches art. 183.1 (dolosa or culposa, any degree of negligence, tipificada and sancionada in this or another law) and art. 184.1 (leve, grave or muy grave per arts. 191 a 206, unitary qualification, proportional fine on the whole base except art. 191.6). |
| after:### Ocultación y medios fraudulentos#1 | FAIL | "y la deuda supera el 10 por ciento de la base de la sanción" drops the qualifier. Art. 184.2 measures "la incidencia de la deuda derivada de la ocultación en relación con la base de la sanción", not the whole debt, which would nearly always exceed 10 % of the base. There is also a stray double comma in "u omisiones,,". |
| after:### Criterios de graduación#1 | FAIL | "lo eleva en 10, 15, 20 o 25 puntos" gives the increments without the brackets that trigger them. Under art. 187.1.b the increment applies only when perjuicio económico is over 10 %, with tiers above 10, 25, 50 and 75 %, so the reader could think any perjuicio adds at least 10 points. Minor point: the denominator also covers "el importe de la devolución inicialmente obtenida". |
| after:### Dejar de ingresar la deuda que debiera resultar de una autoliquidación#1 | PASS | The conduct, the art. 27 and art. 161.1.b exceptions, the leve 50 %, grave 50–100 % and muy grave 100–150 % fines, and the art. 187.1.a and b increments all match art. 191.1–4. The references to arts. 192.1 and 193.1 as separate infracciones are accurate. |
| after:### Obtener indebidamente devoluciones#1 | FAIL | "Pedirla sin obtenerla es otra infracción, la del artículo 194 LGT" drops the condition that limits that infracción. Art. 194.1 sanctions requesting a refund "mediante la omisión de datos relevantes o la inclusión de datos falsos" without obtaining it, not any request for an undue refund. The rest (base, muy grave with medios fraudulentos, 50–100 % and 100–150 % fines with the art. 187.1.a and b increments) matches art. 193. |
| after:### Imputar incorrectamente o no imputar bases imponibles, rentas o resultados en un régimen de imputación de rentas#1 | PASS | Matches art. 196: the entity is the infractor, imputing bases or resultados incorrectly or not at all is grave, there is no infracción for the part already sanctioned under arts. 191, 192 or 193, and the fine is 40 %. |
| after:### Imputar incorrectamente deducciones, bonificaciones y pagos a cuenta en un régimen de imputación de rentas#1 | PASS | Matches art. 197: the same entities, incorrect imputation of deducciones, bonificaciones and pagos a cuenta, grave, the same exception for arts. 191, 192 and 193, and a 75 % fine. |
