## Lesson and norm points for the coordinator

1. **Open item 9 (separación del servicio).** q255 asks which sanction art. 47.1 RD 33/1986 gives to the Consejo de
   Ministros (key: separación del servicio). Art. 61.s) LRJSP now gives «Imponer la sanción de separación del
   servicio por faltas muy graves» to the Ministros. No distractor in q255 becomes true under the LRJSP. The key,
   however, rests on RD 33/1986 art. 47.1 being the rule in force. Keep q255 only if item 9 is settled that way. No
   other question in this part uses «Ministro» as an option for separación.
2. **A second conflict of the same kind.** Art. 63.1.ñ) LRJSP gives the Subsecretario «la potestad disciplinaria …
   por faltas graves o muy graves, salvo la separación del servicio» as his own competence. Art. 47.2 RD 33/1986
   lets him impose suspensión and traslado only «por delegación». For that reason I wrote no question on the
   Subsecretario's standing for suspensión or traslado. q256 asks only what Ministros and Secretarios de Estado
   impose. The ledger could add this to item 9.
3. **Indicios de criminalidad.** Art. 94.3 TREBEP says the tramitación is suspended. Art. 23 RD 33/1986, which the
   lesson does not cite, says the Instructor tells the authority that ordered the incoación and the expediente goes on,
   except for some delitos. q205 and q206 name the TREBEP. Their options avoid «la autoridad que ordenó la incoación»
   and «continúa la tramitación».
4. **DT 3.ª.2 TREBEP.** The lesson quotes only «Grupo A: Subgrupo A1. Grupo B: Subgrupo A2…». q213 asks the Grupo B
   equivalence; C1 and C2 appear only as distractors. I wrote no Instructor-subgroup case, because it would be the
   shape of conv2024_A S1 9 with new figures, and any group below B needs equivalences the lesson does not state.
5. **Month counts and LPAC citations.** The lesson writes «art. 30.2–3 LPAC», which the resolver does not anchor
   (`art. 30.2 LPAC` → None for m04-l06). So the date questions cite the RD 33/1986 or TREBEP article and give the LPAC
   rule only in the explanation. The month counts (pliego, pruebas, 6 months) use art. 30.4 LPAC (date to date; last
   day of the month when the day is missing), which the lesson does not cite. The stems avoid month ends that fall on a
   weekend, so art. 30.5 never decides a key.
6. **No starting day in the norm.** Arts. 37.1, 42 and 45.1 RD 33/1986 do not say from when their plazos run. q243,
   q244 and q254 state the starting day in the stem. Art. 42 («dentro de los diez días siguientes») has no date
   question.
