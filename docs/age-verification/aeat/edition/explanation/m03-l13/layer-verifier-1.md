| caso:### Principio de no concurrencia#1 | FAIL | «Si su comisión repetida ya sirvió para graduar esa sanción, no puede sancionarse además como infracción independiente»: art. 180.1 is about «una misma acción u omisión» that is used as a criterio de graduación or as a circumstance in the calificación. Comisión repetida means earlier infractions that were already sanctioned by a firm resolution (187.1.a). It is not a separate conduct that could be sanctioned on its own. The example tells the reader that repetition could otherwise be its own infraction, which is the wrong rule. A valid example is conduct such as the incumplimiento of facturación (187.1.c), or using false invoices that decide the calificación. The 180.3 part (5.000 sanción + 600 interés both owed) is correct. |
