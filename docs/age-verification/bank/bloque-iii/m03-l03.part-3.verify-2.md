## q262 ruling (art. 46.1 LJCA, acto presunto)

- By the cited text the key holds. Art. 46.1 has not been amended (consolidated text 2025-01-03; LO 1/2025 touched
  arts. 11, 19, 45, 74 and 78) and still says «Si no lo fuera, el plazo será de seis meses».
- The doctrine in force reads it differently. Since STC 52/2014, followed by the TS, the six months do not bar a
  recurso against a desestimación presunta, and arts. 122.1 and 124.1 LPAC allow the administrative recursos «en
  cualquier momento». The stem's «el solicitante» invites that reading. Under it, no option is right. None of the
  three distractors becomes right either. They are 2, 3 and 12 months and none says «sin plazo», so the question
  still has exactly one text-true option and no defensible distractor. Verdict PASS, with the residual risk that a
  candidate challenges the stem's premise.
- The window crosses 24-12-2025 to 6-01-2026, which art. 183 LOPJ (as LO 1/2025 worded it) declares inhábiles «para
  todas las actuaciones judiciales». I took the month plazo as running date to date through it (arts. 5 CC and 185
  LOPJ; art. 128.2 LJCA stops the plazo only in August), so the end date stays mar 19-05-2026. If that period were
  read as suspending the plazo, the end would be 2-06-2026. That date is not an option, so this reading can only
  weaken the key; it cannot make a distractor right.
