## Points for the verifier

- **Short norm names.** The RD 366/2007 full title runs to 33 words, so stems name it «Real Decreto 366/2007, de 16 de
  marzo» (as conv2020_X P1 20 does) or name no norm. The date stems and q050 name RD 951/2005 by number and date to stay
  within 50/40 words. RDL 1/2013 is named by the title of the text it approves («Texto Refundido de la Ley General de
  derechos de las personas con discapacidad y de su inclusión social»).
- **Old organ names.** q050 quotes «de Administraciones Públicas» from art. 17.2 RD 951/2005, the stem saying the
  article «llama» it so (lesson ledger: no «hoy» for this one). No other old name appears.
- **LPAC in explanations.** The date explanations cite art. 30 LPAC for how to count; the question's citation is the
  norm that sets the plazo, which the lesson cites.
- **Official keys reused, distractors not.** q007, q041, q043, q045, q047, q049 keep the official key fact but change
  the angle (whole rule of 10.4; enunciado form; date computation; suspension mechanics; purpose of going to the IGS;
  «tanto centrales como periféricos» against «solo…»). No official distractor is carried into them. q043 avoids 10/15
  días hábiles, un mes and 30 días hábiles; q046 avoids 5 and 2 días hábiles.
- q011 (33 %): options are single minimum figures («el grado mínimo … es»), so a higher figure is not also true.
- q025 cites «art. 10.2.a) Reglamento aprobado por RD 707/2026», the lesson's own form; the explanation says it applies
