## Lesson/norm disagreements

None that changes a fact. Points for the verifier:

- Art. 2.2 Código Civil: the lesson leaves out the middle sentence («La derogación tendrá el alcance que expresamente
  se disponga…»). No question asks it.
- Art. 129.4 LPAC: the lesson quotes the third paragraph without the two incisos the STC 55/2018 annulled («o Consejo
  de Gobierno respectivo», «o de las consejerías del Gobierno»). q036 and q037 ask only what survives. A question on
  the STC itself was written and dropped before hand-back: the lesson states the nullity only as a note, and the
  distractors on the sentence's reach were hard to make wrong by the cited text alone.
- Art. 25.4 Ley 50/1997: the lesson says «El Plan se eleva al Consejo de Ministros antes del 30 de abril»; the norm
  says the Ministro de la Presidencia elevates it. q050 asks the date only.
- Art. 24.1.e) Ley 50/1997: q039 gives the current name (Ministro de la Presidencia, Justicia y Relaciones con las
  Cortes) as the lesson does, with art. 3.1 RD 829/2023 in the explanation. The stem does not quote the norm.
- Explanations of q038, q052 and q053 name the letters of the norm («El artículo 24.1.a)…», «El artículo 26.2.a)…»).
  These are the law's letters, not option letters; options are always named by content.
- Explanations sometimes cite a neighbouring article to say why a distractor is wrong (art. 81.1 CE in q018 for the
  régimen electoral general). Art. 81.1 CE is not in this lesson's prose; the key of q018
  rests on art. 82.1 alone.
