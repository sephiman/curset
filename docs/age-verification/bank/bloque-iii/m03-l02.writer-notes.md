## Date computations

All checked with Python `datetime`. Windows chosen without national or common regional festivos. q049 also says «sin
festivos en el periodo».
- q049 (40.2 + 30.2): dictada martes 10-11-2026; diez hábiles from miércoles 11 → martes 24-11-2026. Distractors: ten
  naturales (viernes 20), five hábiles (martes 17), fifteen hábiles (martes 1-12). The date that counting from the
  day of the act itself would give (lunes 23) is deliberately not an option. Art. 40.2 says «a partir de la fecha en
  que el acto haya sido dictado», and a verifier could read it either way.
- q067 (43.2): puesta a disposición lunes 16-11-2026; last day to access without rejection jueves 26-11-2026 (ten
  naturales from martes 17). The same-day reading (miércoles 25) is not an option, for the same reason.
- q066 (43.2): puesta martes 13-10-2026, acceso miércoles 21 → practicada el 21.
- q059 (41.7): papel lunes 19-10-2026, acceso en sede miércoles 21 → fecha lunes 19. The electronic notification is
  the later one, so the conv2025_X distractor rule («la producida por medios electrónicos») gives a wrong date here.
- q063 (42.2 hours): first attempt lunes 16-11-2026 at 17:00 → second before 15:00; all options are on martes 17. That
  keeps the three-day window and the three-hour margin out of the count.
