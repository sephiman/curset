## Partials and misses

### m03-l03

- 2025 ord. S II 14 — miss. Convenios end by fulfilment of their object or by a cause of resolution: expiry without
  agreed extension, unanimous agreement of all signatories, breach after a formal demand, judicial nullity, or another
  cause in the convenio or the law; a unilateral decision of one party is not one. LRJSP art. 51.1 and 51.2.a)–e).
- 2025 extr. S I 17 — partial. Missing: a first cousin is a fourth-degree collateral blood relative (the abstention rule
  of art. 23.2.b LRJSP is stated). CC art. 918, párrafo tercero.
- 2025 extr. S I res. 5 — miss. The electronic registers of every Administration must be fully interoperable,
  guaranteeing compatibility, interconnection and the telematic transmission of register entries and filed documents.
  LPAC art. 16.4, último párrafo.
- 2025 extr. S II 7 — miss. The statutes of State public bodies are approved by Real Decreto of the Consejo de
  Ministros on the joint proposal of the Ministry of Finance and of the parent Ministry. LRJSP art. 93.2.
- 2025 extr. S II 19 — miss. Within three months of signature, any convenio whose economic commitments exceed 600.000
  euros is sent electronically to the Tribunal de Cuentas (or the regional external audit body). LRJSP art. 53.1.
- 2025 extr. S II res. 5 — miss. In a consortium with AGE participation, creation must be authorised by law, the
  creation convenio needs prior Consejo de Ministros authorisation, and signing it cannot be delegated: it belongs to
  the head of the participating ministerial department (in organismos autónomos, the head of the highest governing
  organ). LRJSP art. 123.2.a)–b).
- 2024 extr. P1 res. 3 — miss. Electronic documents are kept in a format that guarantees authenticity, integrity and
  preservation; the possibility of moving the data to other formats and media must always be ensured; their deletion
  must be authorised under the applicable rules. LPAC art. 17.2.
- 2024 extr. S I 7 — miss. The application must state the electronic means or, failing that, the physical place where
  the party wants to be notified. LPAC art. 66.1.b).
- 2024 extr. S I res. 2 — miss. Those who represent a party obliged to deal electronically are themselves obliged;
  the representative of a non-obliged natural person is not. LPAC art. 14.2.d). Stated in m02-l03, to which m03-l03
  points; not a bracketed alternative.
- 2024 extr. S II 12 — miss. Public employees must deal electronically with the Administration for the steps they take
  as employees. LPAC art. 14.2.e). Stated in m02-l03; not a bracketed alternative.
- 2022 ord. S II 2 — miss. The parties may ask the organ handling a matter to decline its competence and send the file
  to the competent one, or ask the organ they think competent to require the other to inhibit itself. LRJSP art. 14.2.
- 2022 ord. S II 18 — miss (disputed key; judged on the norm). The resolution decides all questions raised and those
  derived from the procedure; on related questions not raised, the organ may rule after putting them to the parties
  for no more than fifteen days to allege and bring evidence. LPAC art. 88.1, párrafo segundo.
- 2022 extr. P1 29 — miss. Sanctioning procedures are always started ex officio by agreement of the competent organ.
  LPAC art. 63.1. Stated in m03-l05; III.3 has no bracketed alternative.
- 2022 extr. S I res. 3 — partial. Same gap as 2025 extr. S I 17: a first cousin is at the fourth degree of
  consanguinity. CC art. 918, párrafo tercero.
- 2022 extr. S II 5 — miss. Absent a period in the sectoral law, very serious infringements prescribe in three years,
  serious ones in two years and minor ones in six months; sanctions for very serious, serious and minor ones in three
  years, two years and one year. LRJSP art. 30.1.

### m03-l04

- 2025 ord. S II 4 — miss. Legal persons may be awarded only contracts whose services fall within the aims, object or
  scope of activity that their statutes or founding rules give them. LCSP art. 66.1.
- 2025 extr. S I 5 — miss. The dynamic purchasing system is a fully electronic process, of limited duration set in the
  pliegos, open throughout its validity to any firm meeting the selection criteria. LCSP art. 223.2.
- 2024 ord. P1 22 — miss. Contracts are awarded using several award criteria based on the best quality-price ratio.
  LCSP art. 145.1, párrafo primero.
- 2024 extr. S II 7 — miss. In works contracts the powers of the responsable del contrato are exercised by the Director
  Facultativo. LCSP art. 62.2 (the duty to appoint a responsable del contrato is art. 62.1).
- 2022 ord. S I 14 — miss. Before lodging the special appeal, those entitled to lodge it may ask the body competent to
  resolve it for interim measures. LCSP art. 49.1.
- 2022 extr. P1 20 — miss. The object of public-sector contracts must be determinate. LCSP art. 99.1.
- 2022 extr. P1 30 — miss. Award is made ordinarily with several criteria based on the best quality-price ratio, by the
  open or restricted procedure. LCSP art. 131.2 (and art. 145.1).
- 2022 extr. P1 res. 5 — miss. Only Spanish or foreign natural or legal persons with full capacity, no prohibition to
  contract and proven solvency (or classification) may contract with the public sector. LCSP art. 65.1.
- 2022 extr. S I res. 4 — partial. Missing: when a contract is harmonised, its tender must also be published in the
  «Diario Oficial de la Unión Europea» (the 140.000-euro services threshold is stated). LCSP art. 135.1, párrafo
  segundo.
- 2022 extr. S II 6 — miss. In works, technical solvency may be shown by the list of works carried out over the last
  five years, backed by certificates of good performance. LCSP art. 88.1.a).
- 2022 extr. S II 16 — miss. The contracting body of the State centralised contracting system is the Junta de
  Contratación Centralizada, attached to the Dirección General de Racionalización y Centralización de la
  Contratación. LCSP art. 229.5 (and art. 323.3).
