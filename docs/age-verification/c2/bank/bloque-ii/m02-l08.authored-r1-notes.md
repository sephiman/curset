## What the first drafts lost, and how this one avoids it

- Every distractor is another real item of the same page (a sibling button, rule, option or row of a table), and the explanation names it by content and says what the page gives it instead. Where the stem asks for a single figure or symbol, the distractors are page figures or are contradicted by the sentence that gives the key.
- Nothing rests on a page's macOS, web or mobile tab. The «nueva fila con Tab» and the «Advertencias, Errores y Sugerencias» facts are only in non-Windows tabs and are not used.
- No shortcut from the US-keyboard page except Ctrl+B for the Navegación pane (the Windows tab of `ms-word-atajos.html`, the same shortcut the 2025 official question uses), with «configurado en español» in the stem and locale-free distractors (Alt+F8, Ctrl+Entrar, F7). The Spanish underline shortcut is not used.
- Stems name Word 365; «configurado en español» where the function name or the shortcut depends on the locale.
- No «cuál … botones» disagreement: stems start «¿Cuál de los siguientes botones…?».
