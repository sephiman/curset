## Detail

- **q266 (check 2).** Key b is the CE 136.1 text, verified. The failure is d. Under LO 2/1982 art. 1.1 the TCu is the supreme fiscalizador of the Estado and the sector público, and art. 4.1.b)–c) include the CCAA and the Corporaciones Locales in that sector público. A candidate who reads LO 2/1982 with CE 136.1 can take d as true, which makes two options correct. Art. 1.2 («sin perjuicio de los órganos fiscalizadores ... de las Comunidades Autónomas») does not exclude d, it only qualifies it. Drop logged to `bloque-v/drops.tsv`, batch `part-3.replacements.verify-1`, check `unique`.
- **q269 (check 5, compared with conv2024_X P1 37).** The official asks for the length of the plazo (seis meses, a partir de la fecha en que se haya rendido, art. 10 LO 2/1982) with four period options. q269 uses the same norm and the same six-month plazo, but its angle is the computed due date, with a weekday and a worked stem. Options are new, and no official distractor (un año / dos años / tres años) is carried over. Under the ruling, «same fact and same angle» is not met, so PASS. The closeness is noted; the coordinator may want to look at it again.
- **q262, q272 (style).** Q262 option a names «el artículo 152.1» inside a statement. That is the norm's own wording and not an article-number option (checklist 4), so PASS.
- **Explanations.** None refers to an option by letter or position (the «c)» and «b)» in q261, q263 and q260 are sub-letters of the norm). Each explanation's count and weekday was checked: q269 180 days = 6-7-2027; q270 60 days = 26-10-2027.
- **Lengths.** Stems: 19–35 words, date stems q269 and q270 at 49 (under the 50 ceiling for date computations). Options 2–15 words; the 2- and 4-word options are organ names, covered by the organ ruling.

## Notes that do not change a verdict

- `m05-l03.md`, section «Fases»: the lesson's list of exclusions from fiscalización previa (art. 151) says «los contratos menores» and omits «asimilados a ellos por la legislación contractual». It is incomplete, not wrong. q260 keys the full phrase.
- The lesson states that «El Tribunal de Cuentas es el supremo órgano fiscalizador ... del Estado, así como del sector público» (art. 136.1 CE) and, in the same section, the LO 2/1982 art. 4 scope. Neither is wrong, but the lesson does not say that the CCAA and the Corporaciones Locales belong to the sector público of LO 2/1982 art. 4. That is the gap q266's option d exploits.
