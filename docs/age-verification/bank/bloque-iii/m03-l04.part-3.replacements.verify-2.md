  - conv2024_A S2 10 vs q283. This is close. The official supuesto has a ministry (Ministerio de Industria y Turismo) whose pliegos a licitador wants to challenge by recurso especial. The question asks which organ resolves it, and the key is the TACRC. q283 has the same fact, the same «which organ» question and the same key. The differences: the contracting body is an organismo autónomo, not a ministry; the stem gives a threshold to check; and the option set is entirely new. The official distractors are invented names («Junta Administrativa…», «Autoridad Central…») and OIReScon. q283's distractors are real organs (the ministry of adscription, the órgano de contratación, the JCCPE). None of them is carried over. That fails neither the spec's definition (same stem with the same option set) nor the ruling, which needs an official distractor carried over. It passes, but only just. If the coordinator wants a stricter reading of «same angle», this is the question to re-check.
