| overview | PASS | Its only legal content is which articles cover which topic (arts. 1-2 objeto y ámbito, 13/15/28/53 derechos, lengua y documentos, 24-25 falta de resolución), and the cited texts match. Everything else describes the lesson's structure. |
| after:## Interesados, capacidad y representación#1 | FAIL | «La tienen quienes la ostentan según las normas civiles; los menores de edad…» reads as the complete list, but art. 3.c) adds the grupos de afectados, uniones y entidades sin personalidad jurídica y patrimonios independientes «cuando la Ley así lo declare expresamente». Also «acredita la representación para solicitar, recurrir, desistir o renunciar» leaves out «presentar declaraciones responsables o comunicaciones», which art. 5.3 also lists. |
| after:## Identificación y firma de los interesados#1 | PASS | Art. 9.1 supports the identity check by nombre y apellidos or denominación as shown in the DNI or an equivalent document. Art. 10.1 supports the definition of signing as showing voluntad y consentimiento. Art. 10.2 supports the electronic signature systems, including the other systems that need registro previo and comunicación previa and take legal effect only two months after that communication. |
| after:## Obligación de resolver#1 | FAIL | «o desde la entrada de la solicitud en el registro» drops the limiting condition in art. 21.3.b): the clock starts on entry «en el registro electrónico de la Administración u Organismo competente para su tramitación». As written, a reader would think filing in any register starts the plazo, which is a common exam trap. |
| after:## Silencio administrativo#1 | FAIL | «En los procedimientos que enumera el artículo el silencio desestima» is stated with no exception. Among the procedures it lists (impugnación de actos), art. 24.1 says that a recurso de alzada filed against a desestimación por silencio «se entenderá estimado» if it is not resolved in time, unless it concerns the excluded matters. The reader would wrongly conclude that silence on an alzada always means rejection. |
| after:## Términos y plazos#1 | PASS | It matches the cited articles. Art. 30.2: plazos by days are días hábiles, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and festivos, unless a Ley or EU law expresses another way of counting. Art. 30.5: a last day that is inhábil carries over to the next día hábil. Art. 31.2.b): a filing on a día inhábil, for plazos in días hábiles, counts as made at the first hour of the next día hábil unless a norma allows reception on días inhábiles. |
