## Notes (do not change a verdict)

- q016 and q030 test the same fact from the same angle as an official question. q016 is close to conv2025_A part 1
  q2 (same stem idea, key «Sancionar y promulgar las leyes»). q030 is close to conv2024_A part 1 q3 (key «Al Rey,
  previa autorización de las Cortes Generales»). Both have new distractor sets, so neither fails the spec's
  definition («same stem with the same option set, or the same options re-ordered»). Still, they are the nearest
  items in the batch to the official texts.
- q003 b («Arbitra y modera el funcionamiento regular de las Cortes Generales») narrows the text rather than
  contradicting it. I kept it as a «texto alterado» distractor because the article names «las instituciones».
- q013: the stem says art. 56.3 «exime del refrendo» what art. 65.2 covers. The text says acts lack validity without
  refrendo «salvo lo dispuesto en el artículo 65, 2». The paraphrase is fair.
- Lesson: I found no sentence at odds with the CE in the parts these questions cite (arts. 1.3, 56, 62–65, 90.2, 91,
  92.2, 99, 100). The lesson's sentence that the 65.2 acts «quedan fuera de la exigencia de refrendo» is faithful.
  Because the lesson says nothing about refrendo for the 65.1 distribution, explanations that assert something
  either way about it have no support (q015).
