## Notes (do not change a verdict)

1. q032, option b). The IV Plan also calls its four principles «ejes»: «pivota en torno a los principios de
   Transparencia, Rendición de Cuentas, Participación e Integridad Pública. Su misión es fortalecer todos y cada uno
   de esos ejes o principios». Option b) lists those four principles, but in the OECD wording («participación de las
   partes interesadas»), and the stem asks for the «cuatro primeros ejes». The Plan uses that same phrase («los cuatro
   primeros ejes del Plan») for the four objectives, so b) is not defensible. A reviewer could still raise the point.
2. q057. Besides the main list, the saved UN page has a navigation menu with other short names («Objetivo 16: Paz y
   justicia», «Objetivo 15: Bosques, desertificación y diversidad biológica»). No option matches the menu's name, so
   the key stays unique. The lesson's list matches the main list on the page.
3. Lesson sentences against the sources: none found at odds. Checked: the IV Plan's four ejes plus the fifth (CCAA
   and FEMP, commitment 10) and its approval on 29-10-2020; the V Plan's approval, 10 commitments, 218 = 123 + 82 + 13
   initiatives, the removal of the ejes and the two cross-cutting ejes; the OGP co-chairing in 2024-2025 (V Plan
   prologue); párrs. 18, 55, 82, 84 and 87 and the preamble of A/RES/70/1; the Objetivo 16 title and targets 15.6,
   16.6, 16.7 and 16.10; the «política palanca» sentence.
