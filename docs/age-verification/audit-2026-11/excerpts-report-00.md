Citation «art. 159.6 LCSP» yields the same text as «art. 159.4.f) LCSP» (item 4): the apartado 1 lead-in plus the ordinal «4.º»
of 159.4.f). The extractor seems to match the first «4.º»/«6.» style marker rather than apartado 6. `excerpt_full` is null, so
nothing behind it either. Item 4 is a yes only because 159.4.f).4.º happens to be what it cites; its lead-in is still the wrong
one (apartado 1 instead of apartado 4 and letra f).

**«Redacción anterior» leaking in unlabeled (future-wording norms)** — flag 33 (m04-l07-q031, art. 19.1 RDL 4/2000). The fact
is in the new wording, so the verdict is yes, but the excerpt ends with the opening quote of the old wording («"1. […]»), and
`excerpt_full` prints the old apartado 1 right after the new one. The BOE's «Téngase en cuenta…» note and the «Redacción
anterior:» label are dropped while the old text is kept, so a reader sees two apartados 1 with nothing to tell them apart.

Not extractor bugs: «prorroga», «comprendera», «considerarase» (items 47, 48) and «distintos, Cuerpos» (46) are in the BOE text
of the TRLCPE.
