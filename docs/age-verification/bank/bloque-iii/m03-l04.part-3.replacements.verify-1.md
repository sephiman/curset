## Notes (do not change a verdict)

- q280 explanation, «no el de un recurso ordinario contra la adjudicación». «Recurso ordinario» is a term of art. Art. 44.5 LCSP uses «recursos administrativos ordinarios» for the recursos that the recurso especial displaces, so calling the recurso especial «ordinario» is imprecise. The sentence «La opción de los días naturales olvida …, y con eso desplaza la regla general de la DA 12.ª» also reads as if the option, not art. 50.1, displaced DA 12.ª. Neither is wrong enough to fail check 4.
- Lesson m03-l04, lines 302–306 (art. 193.3). The lesson is faithful to the LCSP. It does not mention art. 98 RGLCAP (extension of the plazo when the Administración chooses penalidades over resolución). That gap is why q279's distractor looked safe to the writer.
- Lesson lines 406–412 (arts. 45.1, 50.1, 50.2) match the norm.
