  absoluta to lift the veto). q031 asks only who ratifies (the Congreso), as the lesson does.
- Art. 63.2 (tratados): the draft's best distractor depended on art. 94, which the lesson does not cite; dropped rather
  than risk a defensible distractor.
- Art. 115.1, «el decreto de disolución fijará la fecha de las elecciones»: every plausible distractor (fecha de
  constitución de las Cámaras, número de diputados por provincia) is something the convocation decree does fix under
  the LOREG, so no clean question.
- Art. 117.1 (justicia en nombre del Rey), art. 114.1, art. 99.3, art. 62.f second half (empleos, honores), art. 168.3
  and art. 57.1's «misma edad / mismo grado» variant: drafted, cut to stay at 62. Available for replacements.
