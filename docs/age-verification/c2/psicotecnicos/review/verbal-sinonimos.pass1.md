## Defects
- G1 (generator): the explanation template «X significa lo mismo que Y; A y B son opuestas o distintas» names only two of the three distractors (always the first two in option order), never says which one is the antonym and which is unrelated, and agrees masculine adjectives with «opuestas» («sencillo y perpetuo son opuestas»); for a page whose distractors often include the stem's antonym, «opuestas o distintas» is the vague hedge the brief rules out. All 46 single-word items. Suggested fix: name the antonym when there is one («Ortodoxo es su antónimo») and dismiss the rest as «no guardan relación»; quote the words («“sencillo” y “perpetuo”») or say «las palabras … son».
- G2 (generator): distractors are drawn without matching the stem's part of speech; when two or three of them are another category the item collapses to the key (1) or to the key against its antonym (11, 39, 45), which the official items never do (HEREJE: Ortodoxo, Arrepentido, Penitente, Heterodoxo, all the same category). Suggested fix: draw distractors of the stem's part of speech (at least two of three).

Notes (not FAILs): one off-category distractor also appears in 4, 7, 8, 14, 15, 18, 19, 22, 28, 29, 31, 34, 38, 41, 44, 46, 50 (e.g. «Derogar» for PERENNE, «Befa» for LÚCIDO), which makes those items easier than the official ones. Stems repeat a lot in 50 items (LÚCIDO ×6, RECIO ×5, ESCASEZ, LOCUAZ, ATENUAR ×3).
