| after:## Medios de extinción de la deuda#1 | PASS | Matches art. 59.1 and 59.2 LGT, including the liberating effect limited to the amount paid, compensated, deducted or condoned. |
| after:### Legitimación y lugar de pago#1 | PASS | Third-party payment, the third party's lack of standing, the places of payment and the non-liberating payment to a non-competent body or unauthorised person all match art. 33.1–33.3 RGR. |
| after:### Forma de pago#1 | FAIL | The last sentence cites «(art. 60.2 LGT; art. 40.6 RGR)» for «Las deudas que el artículo 65.2 LGT declara inaplazables no admiten pago en especie y esas solicitudes se inadmiten»; art. 40.6 RGR deals with how effective an acceptance is (delivery of the goods, interest) and says nothing about inaplazable debts or inadmission. Only art. 60.2 LGT supports the sentence. |
| after:### Pago en especie#1 | FAIL | «el acuerdo solo es eficaz cuando se entregan los bienes» turns a two-way condition into a single one: art. 40.6 RGR makes the acceptance effective on «la entrega o puesta a disposición de los bienes ofrecidos». The «solo» shuts out the puesta a disposición. The rest (40.1 body, voluntary-period effect with the 161.2 exception, interest, no suspension in the executive period) is correct. |
| after:### Plazos de pago#1 | FAIL | «Cuanto más tarde se notifica, más largo es el plazo» is false and is not in art. 62.2 LGT. Within each bracket a later notification gives less time: a notice on day 1 runs to day 20 of the next month, but one on day 15 has about two weeks less; likewise for days 16 to the last day. Only crossing from day 15 to day 16 lengthens the period. The figures themselves (day 20 of the next month, day 5 of the second month after, next business day, 1 September to 20 November, the two-month minimum) are correct. |
| after:### Suspensión del ingreso sin garantía#1 | PASS | Matches art. 62.8 LGT: it applies only where the tax's own law provides for it, at the debtor's request, total or partial, without a guarantee, and is triggered by the other party's declaration, self-assessment or data communication. The cap is the refund requested, and no late-payment interest is due on the part cancelled against the refund. |
| after:### Consignación del pago#1 | PASS | Matches art. 64 LGT and art. 43.1–43.2 RGR: both cases are given, and so are their effects (suspension if made under the rules on appeals; liberating effect if notified to the collection body). |
| after:### Régimen legal#1 | PASS | Definition and condition match art. 65.1 LGT. The paragraph says the two excluded debts it names are examples from a longer list, and that those requests are declared inadmissible (art. 65.2). |
| after:### Solicitud#1 | PASS | Matches art. 46.1 RGR: voluntary period within the art. 62.1–3 LGT payment period, executive period before notice of the sale agreement. Matches art. 46.6 for the 10-day correction request and the request being treated as never filed and archived. |
| after:### Garantías#1 | PASS | Matches art. 65.3 LGT, art. 48.2 RGR (25 % voluntary, 5 % executive with the surcharge included) and art. 48.6 (two months from the day after notice). The 50 000 € exemption, counting all the debts together, matches art. 2 of Orden HFP/311/2023. |
| after:### Medidas cautelares y dispensa de garantías#1 | PASS | The conditions for substituting precautionary measures, the refusal where the assets can be attached (art. 49.1 RGR) and the investigation of assets in a dispensa (art. 50.1 RGR) match the cited text. «demasiado costosa para la deuda» is a loose paraphrase of «en relación con la cuantía y plazo de la deuda» but does not mislead. |
| after:### Tramitación y resolución#1 | PASS | Matches art. 51.2 RGR (paying the proposed instalments), art. 52.1 (different terms; due dates on day 5 or 20), art. 52.5 (only reposición or REA) and art. 52.6 (six months, then presumed rejection or waiting for an express decision). |
