## Notes (do not change a verdict)

- m01-l09-q062 / check 5, borderline. conv2020 P1 6 has the same article, the same angle and the same stem, here
  recast as a question, and its key is the same verbatim wording. The three distractors are new (Gobierno, Senado, ley
  marco instead of referéndum, tres quintos, ley ordinaria). That is not «the same option set» or «the same options
  re-ordered», so it passes under the spec's wording. A stricter reading of «lightly reworded» would drop it.
- m01-l09-q062, explanation: «los Estatutos se aprueban y se reforman por ley orgánica (art. 81.1)». Art. 81.1 covers
  only approval («las que aprueben los Estatutos de Autonomía»); reform by ley orgánica comes from 147.3, which the
  explanation cites just before. «El Senado solo no aprueba la reforma» reads awkwardly («solo» can be taken two ways),
  but it is not wrong.
- m01-l08-q066, option a is 26 words. It is the art. 59.1 rule in the norm's own wording. The length comes from the
  current ministry name required by checklist 11, and the official conv2024_A reserve 5 option is equally long. I
  applied the «up to 40 when the norm's sentence does» allowance. A count limited to 20 words for a procedure option
  would fail it.
- m01-l03-q064, stem names «la Constitución», not «la Constitución Española de 1978», and gives no article. This
  matches the official form «En relación al funcionamiento de las Cortes Generales recogido en el Título III de la
  Constitución» (conv2022_X P1 19). I took it as the § 2 «statement to complete» form.
- No lesson sentence found at odds with the norm in the passages checked (m01-l03 §§ prerrogativas; m01-l08
  creación de órganos and art. 54.2 «hoy» note; m01-l09 Estatutos, reforma, art. 150).
