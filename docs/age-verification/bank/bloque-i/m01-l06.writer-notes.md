## Blocking: the saved-page citation form is not one the lesson uses

48 of the 61 questions cite a saved page as `fuente: \`file\`` (the spec's form). The lesson never writes
«(fuente: `…`)». It writes the file path as prose inside a parenthetical, e.g. «(Resolución 70/1, párrafo 55;
sources/otros/un-res-70-1-agenda-2030.pdf)». The citation grammar (`backend/src/tradeschool/citations/grammar.py`,
`LEAD` and `_SOURCE`) only reads a segment as a source citation when it starts with `fuente:`, so the lesson
currently yields no `fuente-…` anchors. Until the lesson's parentheticals are rewritten to «(…; fuente:
`un-res-70-1-agenda-2030.pdf`)», the platform guard will reject all 48 questions as citing something their lesson
never cites. The 13 norm citations (`art. 1 Ley 19/2013`, `art. 3.1.c LRJSP`, `art. 3.1.f LRJSP`, `art. 133.1`–`133.4
LPAC`) are written exactly as the lesson writes them and resolve today.

Files cited, all of which the lesson names in prose: `un-res-70-1-agenda-2030.pdf` 17, `transparencia-gobierno-abierto-
que-es.html` 7, `transparencia-v-plan.pdf` 6, `transparencia-iv-plan.pdf` 5, `oecd-recomendacion-gobierno-abierto-2017.pdf`
3, `transparencia-planes-anteriores.html` 3, `transparencia-v-plan.html` 3, `transparencia-planes-accion.html` 2,
`un-ods-objetivos.html` 2.

## Lesson / source disagreements (none changes a key)

1. **V Plan, compromiso 6.** The lesson lists «Cuentas claras y abiertas»; the V Plan PDF («La estructura del V Plan»)
   names it «APERTURA FISCAL: CUENTAS CLARAS Y ABIERTAS». Not asked.
2. **Copresidencia de la OGP.** The lesson says España copresidió el Comité Directivo en 2024-2025 (prólogo). The same
   PDF gives three spans: «(2024-2025)» in the prólogo, «del 1 de octubre de 2023 al 30 de septiembre de 2025» in the
   infographic, and «entre el 1 de octubre de 2024 y el 30 de septiembre de 2025» in the description of the compromisos. Not asked.
3. **Periodo del IV Plan.** `transparencia-planes-anteriores.html` and the IV Plan say 2020-2024; the V Plan PDF once
   says «IV Plan (2019‑2024)». Questions use 2020-2024 and no option uses 2019.
4. **«Cinco ejes» del IV Plan.** The Plan says «cuatro grandes objetivos» plus «un quinto bloque», headed «EJE 5»
   in the body. The lesson's «cinco ejes» matches the headings; q031 and q033 rest on both sentences.
5. **MRI.** The Portal page says «Mecanismo de Revisión Independiente (MRI)», as the lesson; the V Plan PDF says
   «Mecanismo Independiente de Revisión (IRM)». q006 follows the Portal page it cites.
6. **Cinco esferas.** The Resolución lists five headed esferas but never writes «cinco». q049 asks the list, not the
   number.
7. **LPAC art. 133.** STC 55/2018 limits it outside the AGE (note in `normativa.yaml` and the consolidated text). The
   questions ask the article's text, which binds the AGE in full.
